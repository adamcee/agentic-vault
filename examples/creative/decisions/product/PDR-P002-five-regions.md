# PDR-P002: Hollowmere Has Five Regions

> **Superseded 2026-09-08** by [[decisions/product/PDR-P012-three-regions|PDR-P012]] after a scope review showed that five regions would outlast the team's runway.
>
> **Reweave 2026-09-05:** weakened by [[research/production/scope-and-burn]] [emerging]: five regions need about 22 months against 14 months of runway.
>
> **Reweave 2026-09-03:** weakened by [[research/market/publisher-pitch-feedback]] [emerging]: 2 of 3 publishers said five regions is too much for three people by October 2027. Flagged for human review.

## Context
Moon-shift needs room to matter, and the 8–10 hour target needs varied places. Ines sketched Hollowmere as five villages with different trades, factions, and moods ([[world/hollowmere]]).

## Decision
Hollowmere has five regions, each with its own village, letters, and path layouts for every moon phase:
- **Thimble Cross**: a crossroads village of tailors and the tutorial region ([[world/regions/thimble-cross]]).
- **Gloamwick**: a marsh town lit by glow-moss ([[world/regions/gloamwick]]).
- **Sallow Ferry**: a river crossing run by the Ashby family ([[world/regions/sallow-ferry]]).
- **The Drowned Bell**: a flooded chapel village ([[world/regions/cut/the-drowned-bell]]).
- **The High Briar**: thorn uplands ([[world/regions/cut/the-high-briar]]).

## Alternatives Considered
- **Three regions.** The team expected three regions to make an 8-hour game feel thin.
- **One large region.** Moon-shift would carry all the variety, and the setting would grow repetitive.

## Consequences
- Each region needs painted backgrounds for every moon phase, so art cost grows with each region.
- Five regions set the script and art budgets for the greybox and the vertical slice.
