# PDR-P012: Hollowmere Has Three Regions

## Context
[[decisions/product/PDR-P002-five-regions|PDR-P002]] planned five regions. Two pieces of evidence in one week showed that five regions do not fit three people and an October 2027 launch.

## What Changed
On 2026-09-03, 2 of 3 publishers said five regions is too much for three people by October 2027 ([[research/market/publisher-pitch-feedback]] [emerging]). On 2026-09-05, the scope review put runway at 14 months, with five regions needing about 22 months and three needing about 13 ([[research/production/scope-and-burn]] [emerging]).

## Decision
Hollowmere has three regions: Thimble Cross, Gloamwick, and Sallow Ferry ([[world/regions/thimble-cross]], [[world/regions/gloamwick]], [[world/regions/sallow-ferry]]). The Drowned Bell and the High Briar are cut.
- **The Drowned Bell**: its best story, the Tolley letters, moves to Sallow Ferry ([[world/regions/cut/the-drowned-bell]]).
- **The High Briar**: its thorn uplands fold into Gloamwick's edges ([[world/regions/cut/the-high-briar]]).

## Alternatives Considered
- **Keep five regions and seek publisher money first.** Two publishers named scope as the problem, so funding would not fix it.
- **Keep five smaller regions.** Each region still needs painted backgrounds for every moon phase, so art cost barely shrinks.
- **Delay launch past October 2027.** It breaks clause P4 of [[architecture/CONSTITUTION]] and outruns the money.

## Consequences
- Three regions fit in about 13 of the 14 months of runway, which leaves about one month of margin.
- Each remaining region carries more letters, so the 8–10 hour target depends on depth instead of breadth.
- **Reweave 2026-09-10:** strengthened by [[research/narrative/script-word-count]] [emerging]: the planned script drops from 142,000 words for five regions to about 86,000 for three.
