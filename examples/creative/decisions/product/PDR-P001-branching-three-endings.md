# PDR-P001: Branching Story with Three Endings

> **Superseded on 2026-09-24** by [[decisions/product/PDR-P005-linear-story-local-choices|PDR-P005]]. Kept for the record.

## Context
Low Tide follows Ines, who returns to her late father's tidal island to recover his lost logbook. Mara wanted the player's choices about the logbook to shape how the story ends.

## Decision
The story branches at the end of act 1 into three routes, each with its own ending. Routes share puzzles but differ in scenes, dialogue, and some backgrounds.

## Alternatives Considered
- **Linear story.** Simpler to build, but Mara felt it would make choices feel empty.
- **One ending with variations.** A single final scene with lines that change based on earlier choices.

## Consequences
- **More writing and art.** Three routes need roughly twice the scenes of a linear story [unsourced].
- **Replay value.** The design assumes players replay to see other endings [unsourced].

## Reweave Notes
- **2026-09-18, weakened.** 5 of 6 playtesters said they would not replay to see another ending ([[research/playtest-round-1]] [emerging]). Flagged for Mara and Teo.
- **2026-09-21, invalidated.** The branching script needs about 95,000 words and 62 backgrounds, more than two people can finish in the 30 weeks left ([[research/scope-estimate]] [emerging]). Proposed a superseding PDR.
