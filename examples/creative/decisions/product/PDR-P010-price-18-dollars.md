# PDR-P010: Launch Price Is $18

## Context
Night Post runs 8–10 hours. Eight comparable narrative adventures run 5–12 hours and launched at $15–$25, with a median of $19.99 ([[research/market/genre-comparables]] [emerging]).

In the team's own survey, 44% of 214 respondents said they would buy at $18 or less ([[research/market/community-survey-01]] [emerging]). The survey drew on the devlog newsletter and community server, so it leans toward people who already follow the game.

## Decision
Night Post launches at $18. It has no microtransactions, in line with clause P3 of [[architecture/CONSTITUTION]].

## Alternatives Considered
- **$19.99, the comparables' median.** It matches the market, but it sits above the $18 line that the survey used.
- **$15.** It earns less per sale, and Night Post's length sits in the middle of the comparables' range, not at the short end.
- **$25.** It is the top of the range, and a first release from an unknown team would struggle to justify it.

## Consequences
- Storefront regional pricing will lower the effective price in Latin American markets, where much of the Spanish-speaking audience lives [unsourced].
- Revenue estimates in [[strategy/release-plan]] and [[strategy/funding]] use $18.
- The price rests on two [emerging] sources, so it stays open to change until the store page goes live.
- **Reweave 2026-10-06:** strengthened by [[research/market/demo-festival-results]] [emerging]: wishlists rose from 1,900 to 6,450 during the festival, and 41% of 3,140 demo players finished the demo.
