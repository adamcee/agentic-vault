# PDR-P005: Linear Story with Small Local Choices

## Context
This record supersedes [[decisions/product/PDR-P001-branching-three-endings|PDR-P001]]. Two pieces of evidence changed the picture after PDR-P001 was written:
- **Players don't replay.** 5 of 6 round 1 playtesters would not replay to see another ending ([[research/playtest-round-1]] [emerging]).
- **Branching does not fit two people.** Three routes need about 95,000 words and 62 backgrounds, against capacity for about 45,000 words and 600 art hours ([[research/scope-estimate]] [emerging]).

## Decision
Low Tide tells one linear story with one ending. Choices are [[concepts/local-choice|local choices]]: they change a scene or the next one, never the ending.

## Alternatives Considered
- **Keep three endings, cut puzzles.** Saves art but removes the game's core.
- **One ending with variations.** Cheaper than routes, but still multiplies the final act's script.
- **No choices.** Simplest, but round 1 players liked choosing what to do with the logbook.

## Consequences
- **Fits the schedule.** The linear script needs about 40,000 words and 30 backgrounds ([[research/scope-estimate]] [emerging]).
- **Replay is not a goal.** Store pages and the strategy drop any promise of multiple endings ([[strategy/goals-and-audience]]).
- **Branch scenes are cut.** 17 drafted scenes leave the script and go to the cut-content folder.

## Reweave Notes
- **2026-10-05, strengthened.** 7 of 8 round 2 playtesters would not replay, which repeats round 1 ([[research/playtest-round-2]] [supported]). 6 of 8 named a local choice as a highlight, and none asked about other endings ([[research/playtest-round-2]] [emerging]).
