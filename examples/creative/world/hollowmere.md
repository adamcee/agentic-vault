# Hollowmere

Hollowmere is a forest whose paths rearrange with the phase of the moon, and it is the whole setting of Night Post. Its villages can reach each other only after dark, which is why letters travel by night courier.

> "Ask the road where it goes tonight, not where it went last night."
> — a Thimble Cross saying

## The Forest's Rules

- **Paths connect only at night.** By day, every path out of a village loops back to where it started. After dusk, the paths reach other villages, and nobody in Hollowmere knows why.
- **The moon decides the routes.** Each night's moon phase sets which paths join which waymarks. Players call this the [[concepts/moon-shift]], and the rules live in [[architecture/systems/moon-and-paths]].
- **Waymarks hold still.** A [[concepts/waymark]] is a lantern post where paths meet. The paths move between waymarks, but the waymarks themselves never move.
- **Thimble Cross never shifts.** Its crossroads holds the same four roads under every moon, which is why the Night Post built its office there ([[world/regions/thimble-cross]]).

## Living Things

- **Glow-moss.** A pale green moss that lights Gloamwick's streets. It dims when someone lies nearby, which the Hushfolk find funny.
- **Tallow-reed.** A marsh reed the Lamplighters' Union renders into lantern oil. Whoever controls the reed beds controls the price of light ([[concepts/lantern-oil]]).
- **Hollow oaks.** Old oaks with rooms inside the trunk. The Hushfolk gather in them between waymarks.

## Creatures Wren Parleys With

Creatures block paths, but none of them want to hurt Wren. Each one wants something, and Wren settles it by [[concepts/parley]]: talking, offering an item from the satchel, or shining the lantern.

- **The Tallow Stag.** A white stag that stands in lantern light and won't move until the light moves first.
- **Mire-geese.** Territorial geese on Gloamwick's causeways that let Wren pass for a crust of bread or a good insult.
- **The briar hound.** A thorn-coated dog on the Briar Edge that guards lost things and gives one back to anyone who can name its owner.

## Design Notes

- **Authored paths, not generated ones.** Every path is hand-placed so letters and landmarks sit where the writer put them ([[decisions/rejected/ADR-T004-procedural-forest|ADR-T004]]).
- **Moon-shifting paths stay, with fixed landmarks.** Playtest 4 showed players getting lost, so waymarks gained landmarks that never change ([[decisions/product/PDR-P004-moon-shifting-paths|PDR-P004]], [[research/playtests/pt-04-vertical-slice]] [emerging]).
- **Painted night palette.** The forest is painted 2D in a limited night palette ([[decisions/product/PDR-P005-painted-night-palette|PDR-P005]]).
- **Creatures replaced combat.** Creatures were turn-based enemies until [[decisions/product/PDR-P009-parley-encounters|PDR-P009]] replaced that with parley.
