# Hollowmere Timeline

Hollowmere's history turns on one night sixty-one years before the game, when an unpaid oil bill left the river path dark and the Drowned Bell flooded. Most of the debts and grudges in the game's letters trace back to it.

Dates count back from the autumn Wren arrives. Villagers date things by Post seasons, not by calendar years.

> "The Post owes the Union for oil, the Union owes the Ashbys for silence, and the Ashbys owe the Tolleys for a night nobody will talk about."
> — Ledger, to Wren

## Before the Settlers

- **Time out of mind.** The Hushfolk live in the hollow oaks. The paths already move with the moon ([[world/factions/the-hushfolk]]).

## Settlement and Institutions

- **About 300 years before.** Settlers arrive along the river Sallow and find that the crossroads at Thimble Cross holds under every moon. The village grows around it ([[world/regions/thimble-cross]]).
- **About 200 years before.** Marsh families in Gloamwick learn to render tallow-reed into oil and light the first waymark lanterns. They form the Lamplighters' Union ([[world/factions/lamplighters-union]]).
- **140 years before.** The tailors' guild of Thimble Cross founds the Night Post in its old guildhall, now called the Sorting House ([[world/factions/the-night-post]]).
- **About 100 years before.** The Post and the Hushfolk agree the Hush Accord: the Hushfolk guide lost couriers home, and each courier pays with one true thing ([[world/characters/mothwife-sel]]).

## The Dark Night

- **61 years before.** Postmaster Gideon Marr refuses the Union's new oil price. The Union stops lighting the river-path lanterns until the Post pays.
- **61 years before, the same autumn.** The river Sallow floods the chapel village later called the Drowned Bell. The bell-ringer Maud Tolley rings the bell all night so others can find the way out, and she does not survive ([[world/regions/cut/the-drowned-bell]]).
- **That night.** Ferryman Amos Ashby, Bram's grandfather, takes survivors across the dark river. The Tolleys believe he refused to go back for Maud ([[world/regions/sallow-ferry]]).
- **That night.** A young lamplighter obeys the Union's order to leave the river lantern dark. That lamplighter has not spoken since ([[world/characters/the-quiet-lamplighter]]).

## After the Flood

- **60 years before.** Drowned Bell survivors settle in the three remaining villages. The Union bills the Post every autumn, and the Post never pays.
- **About 30 years before.** Smugglers start running untaxed oil down the Sallow, and the Ashby ferry looks the other way for a fee.
- **25 years before.** A young crow named Ledger begins flying routes for the Post ([[world/characters/ledger]]).
- **18 years before.** Gideon Marr dies. His daughter Odile becomes Postmistress and keeps refusing the oil bill ([[world/characters/odile-marr]]).
- **3 years before.** Ledger retires to the rafters of the Sorting House.

## The Game

- **The first night of the autumn season.** Wren arrives in Thimble Cross as the newest courier ([[world/characters/wren]]). The Union's sixty-first unpaid oil bill sits in the Sorting House, waiting to be delivered.
