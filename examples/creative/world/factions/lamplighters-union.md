# The Lamplighters' Union

The Lamplighters' Union keeps Hollowmere's waymark lanterns lit, renders the oil that fuels them, and is at odds with the Night Post over an oil bill unpaid for sixty-one years. Its hall, the Wick House, stands in Gloamwick.

> "We light your way. Kindly pay."
> — painted on every Union lantern post

## What It Is

- **Founded.** Marsh families in Gloamwick formed the Union about 200 years before the game, when they learned to render tallow-reed into oil ([[world/timeline]]).
- **What it does.** Union lamplighters walk the forest at dusk and light every [[concepts/waymark]] lantern, including the red and green route lanterns.
- **Who leads it.** A council of elders, of whom the Quiet Lamplighter is the oldest and the most respected ([[world/characters/the-quiet-lamplighter]]).
- **Where it lives.** The Wick House in [[world/regions/gloamwick]], built around the rendering vats.

## How It Shows Up in Play

- **Sells oil.** The Union sells [[concepts/lantern-oil]] to Wren, and its price depends on how Wren has treated the Union, including what happened to the Oil Bill.
- **Lights the waymarks.** A waymark the Union has lit is a safe place to stop and check the route ([[concepts/moon-shift]]).
- **Writes letters.** Union letters carry the oil dispute, the smuggling problem, and the Quiet Lamplighter's conversations with Wren.

## Relationships

- **With the Night Post.** The Union bills the Post every autumn and the Post never pays. Sixty-one years ago, in protest, the Union stopped lighting the river-path lanterns, and the Drowned Bell flooded that same autumn ([[world/factions/the-night-post]], [[world/regions/cut/the-drowned-bell]]).
- **With the Ashbys.** The Union knows smugglers run untaxed oil down the Sallow past the Ashby ferry, and it suspects Bram takes a cut ([[world/characters/bram-ashby]]).
- **With the Hushfolk.** Polite and distant ([[world/factions/the-hushfolk]]).

## Design Notes

- **Oil as clock and currency.** The Union's quarrel with the Post gives the oil economy a story reason ([[architecture/systems/lantern-oil-economy]]).
- **No villain.** The Union darkened a lantern over an unpaid bill, and the Post refused a price it called unfair. Both sides had a reason, which keeps the story inside the tone set in [[decisions/product/PDR-P001-letters-are-the-quests|PDR-P001]].
