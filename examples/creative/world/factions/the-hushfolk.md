# The Hushfolk

The Hushfolk are moth-like forest folk who trade in secrets and never lie. They lived in Hollowmere before any village, and they treat what Wren learns from letters as currency.

> "A moth says what is so, or says nothing."
> — Hushfolk saying

## What They Are

- **Appearance.** Person-sized, with soft grey wings folded like a cloak and feathered antennae. They gather in hollow oaks between waymarks and come out to lantern light ([[world/hollowmere]]).
- **They never lie.** Every statement a Hushfolk makes is true. They can refuse to answer, answer only the exact question asked, or stay silent, and they use all three.
- **They trade in secrets.** A Hushfolk will pay for a true thing it didn't know. Payment is usually a path, a warning, or a secret about someone else.

## How They Show Up in Play

- **Secrets as parley offers.** In a [[concepts/parley]], Wren can offer a Hushfolk something learned from an opened [[concepts/sealed-letter]]. This is how reading letters pays off outside the letter's own story.
- **Guides when lost.** Under the Hush Accord, a Hushfolk will show a lost courier the way home in exchange for one true thing ([[world/factions/the-night-post]]).
- **Envoy.** Mothwife Sel speaks for the Hushfolk at the Sorting House and is the one Wren meets most ([[world/characters/mothwife-sel]]).

## Relationships

- **With the Night Post.** Bound by the Hush Accord for about 100 years ([[world/timeline]]).
- **With the Lamplighters' Union.** Polite and distant. The Hushfolk avoid Gloamwick's brightest streets because glow-moss dims near lies, and the villagers keep checking it when a Hushfolk speaks ([[world/regions/gloamwick]]).
- **With the villages.** Villagers sell them secrets about each other, which keeps old grudges alive.

## Design Notes

- **Canon over drafts.** [[drafts/ai-lore-hushfolk-expansion]] is AI-generated lore that says the Hushfolk can lie. That contradicts this file, so the draft stays in `drafts/` until Ines reviews it ([[world/README]]).
- **Why they never lie.** A faction that only tells the truth makes reading letters valuable: Wren can confirm or disprove what a letter claims by asking a Hushfolk.
