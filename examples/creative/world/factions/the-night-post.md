# The Night Post

The Night Post is Hollowmere's courier service, which carries letters between villages after dark, and it is the institution Wren works for. It is bureaucratic and proud, and it has refused to pay the Lamplighters' Union's oil bill for sixty-one years.

> "Through dark, by dawn, unopened."
> — the Night Post motto, carved over the Sorting House door

## What It Is

- **Founded.** The tailors' guild of Thimble Cross founded the Post 140 years before the game, in the guildhall now called the Sorting House ([[world/timeline]], [[world/regions/thimble-cross]]).
- **Who runs it.** Postmistress Odile Marr runs the office and hands out each night's letters ([[world/characters/odile-marr]]).
- **Who works for it.** A handful of couriers in grey coats sewn by Coombe & Daughter, sorters who work by day, and Ledger, a retired crow ([[world/characters/ledger]]).
- **Its rules.** The Post Rulebook covers everything from coat buttons to route logs. Rule One says a courier never opens a sealed letter.

## How It Shows Up in Play

- **Letters start and end here.** Wren picks up letters at dusk and returns by dawn, as in the core loop of [[architecture/game-design-overview]].
- **Postmarks are the Post's judgement.** The office stamps each delivery by how Wren made it: fast, careful, kind, or nosy ([[concepts/postmark]]).
- **Rule One is breakable.** Wren can open any [[concepts/sealed-letter]]. The Post notices through nosy postmarks and through what recipients say, but it never fires Wren ([[decisions/product/PDR-P008-sealed-letters-can-be-opened|PDR-P008]]).

## Relationships

- **With the Lamplighters' Union.** The Post's couriers rely on waymark lanterns the Union lights, yet the Post calls the Union's oil price robbery and won't pay. The quarrel began under Odile's father, Gideon Marr ([[world/factions/lamplighters-union]]).
- **With the Hushfolk.** Under the Hush Accord, the Hushfolk guide lost couriers home and each courier pays with one true thing. Mothwife Sel is the Hushfolk envoy to the Post ([[world/factions/the-hushfolk]], [[world/characters/mothwife-sel]]).
- **With the Ashbys.** The Post pays the Ashby ferry a standing fare for couriers and pretends not to know about the smuggling ([[world/regions/sallow-ferry]]).

## Design Notes

- **A courier, not a warrior.** The Post exists so every quest can be a letter ([[decisions/product/PDR-P001-letters-are-the-quests|PDR-P001]]).
- **Solitary by design.** Wren works alone; two-player co-op was rejected partly because it breaks this ([[decisions/rejected/PDR-P007-co-op-courier-mode|PDR-P007]]).
