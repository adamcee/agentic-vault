# The Quiet Lamplighter

The Quiet Lamplighter is the oldest elder of the Lamplighters' Union in Gloamwick, has no name in the game, and has not spoken aloud in sixty-one years. The elder talks to Wren only by letter, and those are the only letters in the game addressed to Wren.

> "You carry other people's words all night. Here are a few of mine. Don't hurry with them."
> — the Quiet Lamplighter's first letter to Wren

## Who the Elder Is

- **The young lamplighter of the Dark Night.** Sixty-one years ago, the elder obeyed the Union's order to leave the river lantern dark during the oil dispute. The Drowned Bell flooded that night, and the elder has not spoken since ([[world/timeline]]).
- **Respected and unknown.** Nobody under sixty has heard the elder's voice, and the Union uses no other name. Villagers say "the Quiet one" ([[world/factions/lamplighters-union]]).
- **Still at work.** The elder keeps a desk in the Wick House loft and still lights one lantern every night: the river lantern above Sallow Ferry.

## What the Player Does With the Elder

- **Exchanges letters.** Wren receives a letter from the elder on several nights and can write a short reply chosen from options. The thread is a slow conversation across the game.
- **Learns the Union's side.** The letters explain the oil dispute from inside the Union, without excuses.
- **Can bring the Tolley story home.** If Wren has read the Tolley letters, the elder's last letter admits the elder's part in the dark river ([[world/regions/sallow-ferry]]).

## Relationships

- **[[world/characters/wren]]**: the only courier the elder has written to.
- **[[world/characters/odile-marr]]**: one formal letter a year, exchanged without warmth.
- **June Tolley**: a young lamplighter the elder trained, who doesn't know the elder's part in her grandmother's death.

## Design Notes

- **Silence makes letters the dialogue.** An elder who speaks only in letters keeps the character inside the letter format of [[decisions/product/PDR-P001-letters-are-the-quests|PDR-P001]], and each letter respects the [[concepts/letter-budget]].
- **Readable at handheld size.** The elder's letters use the handwritten style for header and signature only, with a readable body ([[decisions/product/PDR-P011-readable-letters|PDR-P011]], [[architecture/ui/letter-reader]]).
