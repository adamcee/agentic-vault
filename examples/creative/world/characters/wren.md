# Wren

Wren is the player character: the newest courier of the Night Post, arriving in Thimble Cross on the first night of the autumn season. Wren is new to Hollowmere, so the player and Wren learn the forest together.

> "New one, is it? Keep your lantern high and your thumbs off the seals."
> — Odile Marr, on Wren's first night

## Who Wren Is

- **A stranger.** Wren comes from outside the forest and has no family ties to any village or faction. Villagers judge Wren only by deliveries.
- **A courier.** Wren carries a satchel, a lantern, and a grey coat from Coombe & Daughter. Wren has no weapon.
- **Defined by choices.** Wren's personality comes from how the player delivers. The [[concepts/postmark]] stamps record whether Wren is fast, careful, kind, or nosy.

## What Wren Does

- **Delivers letters.** Every quest is a letter ([[decisions/product/PDR-P001-letters-are-the-quests|PDR-P001]]).
- **Plans routes by moon.** Wren reads the night's moon phase and plans a path between waymarks ([[concepts/moon-shift]], [[concepts/waymark]]).
- **Manages oil and weight.** [[concepts/lantern-oil]] sets how long Wren can stay out, and [[concepts/satchel-weight]] trades how much Wren carries against how fast Wren walks.
- **Parleys.** Wren settles every encounter by talking, offering an item, or shining the lantern ([[concepts/parley]]).
- **Chooses whether to open letters.** Opening a [[concepts/sealed-letter]] changes what people say to Wren and what happens next.

## Relationships

- **[[world/characters/odile-marr]]** is Wren's employer, strict about the rules and quietly glad of a new courier.
- **[[world/characters/ledger]]** is Wren's scout and running commentary.
- **[[world/characters/mothwife-sel]]** buys what Wren learns.
- **[[world/characters/the-quiet-lamplighter]]** writes letters addressed to Wren, the only ones in the game.
- **[[world/characters/bram-ashby]]** ferries Wren and has unsent letters Wren may find.

## Design Notes

- **Wren cannot die.** Encounters have no health and no death ([[decisions/product/PDR-P009-parley-encounters|PDR-P009]], [[architecture/CONSTITUTION]] P1).
- **Works alone.** There is no second courier for a co-op partner ([[decisions/rejected/PDR-P007-co-op-courier-mode|PDR-P007]]).
- **Speaks through choices.** Wren talks through dialogue choices in text, and only short barks are voiced across the cast ([[decisions/rejected/PDR-P013-full-voice-acting|PDR-P013]]).
