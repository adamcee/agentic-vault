# Bram Ashby

Bram Ashby is the ferryman at Sallow Ferry, head of the Ashby family, and the man at the centre of the Tolley letters. He takes a fee to look the other way when smugglers cross, and he writes letters of apology he never sends.

> "June, I've started this letter nine times. My grandfather went back. I know you won't believe that from an Ashby."
> — unsent letter from Bram Ashby, found in the Ferry House

## Who Bram Is

- **A ferryman.** Bram rows the Ashby ferry across the river Sallow every night, as his father and grandfather did ([[world/regions/sallow-ferry]]).
- **A small-time accomplice.** He lets smugglers move untaxed oil across the river and takes a cut. He tells himself the Union's price is the real crime ([[world/factions/lamplighters-union]]).
- **Carrying his grandfather's debt.** The Tolleys believe Amos Ashby refused to row back for Maud Tolley on the night the Drowned Bell flooded. Bram has never found the courage to answer them ([[world/timeline]]).

## What the Player Does With Him

- **Pays the fare.** Bram's fare changes with what Wren knows about him, which is a [[concepts/parley]] in all but name.
- **Finds his unsent letters.** Wren can find Bram's letters to June Tolley in the Ferry House and choose whether to carry them.
- **Reads Amos's fare ledger.** The ledger in the attic shows Amos went back for Maud and couldn't find her in the dark.

## Relationships

- **June Tolley**: a lamplighter in [[world/regions/gloamwick]] who writes to him asking for an apology.
- **[[world/characters/wren]]**: a courier who might carry his letters, or read them.
- **[[world/characters/odile-marr]]**: the Post pays him a standing fare and doesn't ask about the crates.

## Design Notes

- **Story moved from a cut region.** Bram's role in the Tolley story came from [[world/regions/cut/the-drowned-bell]] when [[decisions/product/PDR-P012-three-regions|PDR-P012]] cut it.
- **Voiced barks only.** Bram's ferry calls are among the short voiced barks kept after full voice acting was rejected ([[decisions/rejected/PDR-P013-full-voice-acting|PDR-P013]]).
