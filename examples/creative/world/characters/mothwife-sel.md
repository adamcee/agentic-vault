# Mothwife Sel

Mothwife Sel is the Hushfolk envoy to the Night Post, who buys secrets from Wren and pays in paths, warnings, and other people's secrets. Like all Hushfolk, Sel never lies, and Sel is very good at not answering.

> "I did not say she was innocent. I said she was not there. Those are different words, courier."
> — Mothwife Sel

## Who Sel Is

- **The envoy.** Sel speaks for the Hushfolk under the Hush Accord and visits the Sorting House on nights the moon is thin ([[world/factions/the-hushfolk]]).
- **"Mothwife" is a title.** The Hushfolk give it to whoever keeps the accord, and Sel has held it longer than Odile has been Postmistress.
- **Curious, not kind.** Sel likes Wren because Wren brings new secrets. Sel will warn Wren of trouble only if the warning is paid for.

## What the Player Does With Sel

- **Trades secrets.** Wren can sell Sel something learned from an opened [[concepts/sealed-letter]], and Sel pays with a route tip or a secret about another character.
- **Checks the truth.** Because Sel never lies, Wren can ask Sel whether a letter's claim is true. Sel answers exactly the question asked and nothing more.
- **Gets guided home.** If Wren gets lost far from Thimble Cross, Sel can lead Wren back in exchange for one true thing, which saves [[concepts/lantern-oil]] that wandering would burn.

## Relationships

- **[[world/characters/wren]]**: Sel's best source in years.
- **[[world/characters/odile-marr]]**: an old trading partner who doesn't like how much Sel knows about the Marrs.
- **[[world/characters/ledger]]**: Ledger finds Sel tiresome, and Sel finds that funny.
