# Odile Marr

Postmistress Odile Marr runs the Night Post office in Thimble Cross and hands Wren each night's letters. She inherited the office and the oil quarrel from her father, Gideon Marr, and she defends both out of pride.

> "The Post does not pay robbers, and the Post does not apologise. File it."
> — Odile Marr, on the Oil Bill

## Who Odile Is

- **A former courier.** Odile walked routes with Ledger before she took over the office ([[world/characters/ledger]]).
- **Postmistress for eighteen years.** She took over the Sorting House when Gideon Marr died ([[world/timeline]]).
- **Strict and proud.** She enforces the Post Rulebook to the letter and keeps every route log since the Post's founding.
- **Hiding a doubt.** Odile suspects her father's refusal to pay for oil left the river lantern dark on the night the Drowned Bell flooded. She has never said so aloud.

## What the Player Does With Her

- **Gets letters at dusk.** Each night begins at her counter in the Sorting House ([[world/regions/thimble-cross]]).
- **Reports at dawn.** She logs deliveries and hands out [[concepts/postmark]] stamps.
- **Carries the Oil Bill, or doesn't.** Odile keeps the Union's sixty-first unpaid bill on her desk. What Wren does with it, and with the Union's reply, decides whether Odile ever admits her doubt ([[world/factions/lamplighters-union]]).

## Relationships

- **[[world/characters/wren]]**: her newest courier.
- **[[world/characters/the-quiet-lamplighter]]**: the two have never spoken, which is easy since the elder doesn't speak. They exchange formal letters once a year.
- **[[world/characters/mothwife-sel]]**: Odile trades with Sel under the Hush Accord and dislikes how much Sel knows about the Marr family.

## Design Notes

- **No villain.** Odile is stubborn, not cruel; her refusal comes from a family grudge, which fits the tone of [[decisions/product/PDR-P001-letters-are-the-quests|PDR-P001]].
- **Social consequences only.** If Wren opens letters, Odile's trust changes, but she never fires Wren ([[decisions/product/PDR-P008-sealed-letters-can-be-opened|PDR-P008]]).
