# Ledger

Ledger is a retired postal crow who scouts ahead for Wren and comments on everything. He flew routes for the Night Post for twenty-two years and retired three years before the game to the rafters of the Sorting House.

> "That seal's been steamed before, you know. Not by me. Probably."
> — Ledger

## Who Ledger Is

- **A retired professional.** Ledger flew routes for the Post from 25 years before the game until he retired ([[world/timeline]]). He flew with Odile Marr when she was a courier, and he still calls her "the girl" ([[world/characters/odile-marr]]).
- **A gossip.** Ledger has read more letters over Wren's shoulder than any courier has opened. He knows who owes whom and enjoys saying so.
- **Not quite retired.** He tells everyone he has retired and then follows Wren out every night.

## What Ledger Does in Play

- **Scouts.** Ledger flies ahead to show where the paths lead under tonight's moon ([[concepts/moon-shift]], [[architecture/systems/moon-and-paths]]).
- **Comments.** He reacts to letters, deliveries, and Wren's choices, which gives the player a second voice on long walks.
- **Remembers history.** Ledger knows the outline of the Dark Night and the oil dispute, though not the whole truth of either ([[world/timeline]]).

## Relationships

- **[[world/characters/wren]]**: his new courier, whom he treats as a trainee.
- **[[world/characters/odile-marr]]**: his old partner on the routes. They bicker like siblings.
- **[[world/characters/mothwife-sel]]**: Ledger can't stand Hushfolk honesty, because it spoils his stories.

## Design Notes

- **Open idea.** [[ideas/ledger-upgrades]] proposes new scouting abilities for Ledger as the game goes on.
- **A companion, not a co-op partner.** Ledger gives Wren company without a second player ([[decisions/rejected/PDR-P007-co-op-courier-mode|PDR-P007]]).
