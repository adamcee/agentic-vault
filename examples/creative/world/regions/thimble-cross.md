# Thimble Cross

Thimble Cross is a crossroads village of tailors built around the Night Post office, and it is where every night of the game begins and ends. It is the tutorial region and the hub Wren returns to at dawn.

> "Measure twice, seal once."
> — sign over the Sorting House counter

## What the Player Does Here

- **Picks up letters at dusk.** Postmistress Odile Marr hands out the night's letters at the Sorting House counter ([[world/characters/odile-marr]]). This is the first step of the core loop in [[architecture/game-design-overview]].
- **Learns to plan a route.** The crossroads holds the same four roads under every moon, so the first lesson in [[concepts/moon-shift]] happens one road out, where the paths start moving.
- **Opens a first sealed letter.** The tutorial puts a [[concepts/sealed-letter]] in Wren's satchel early, so the player sees that opening one changes how people talk to Wren.
- **Returns by dawn.** Wren comes back to the Sorting House to log deliveries and receive [[concepts/postmark]] stamps.

## Places

- **The Sorting House.** The Night Post office, a former tailors' guildhall with pigeonholes where the bolts of cloth used to sit. Ledger roosts in its rafters ([[world/characters/ledger]]).
- **Coombe & Daughter.** Hettie Coombe's tailoring shop, which sews every courier's grey coat.
- **The Crossroads Stone.** A worn milestone at the centre of the crossing, carved with the names of couriers who never came back.

## Letters and Relationships

- **The Button Letter.** In the tutorial letter, Pim, Hettie Coombe's apprentice, sends a love letter and one coat button to Lissa, a glow-moss gatherer in Gloamwick. If Wren opens it, Wren learns Pim has been taking buttons from Hettie's stock and can tell her or keep quiet.
- **The Oil Bill.** The Union's sixty-first unpaid bill to the Post sits on Odile's desk. Whether Wren carries it to Gloamwick, and whether Wren reads it first, shapes how the Union treats Wren ([[world/factions/lamplighters-union]]).
- **Letters from the drowned families.** Several tailors descend from Drowned Bell survivors, and their letters seed the Tolley story that pays off at [[world/regions/sallow-ferry]].

## Connections

- **East to Gloamwick.** The marsh road leads to [[world/regions/gloamwick]] and the Lamplighters' Union.
- **South to Sallow Ferry.** The river road leads to [[world/regions/sallow-ferry]] and the Ashby ferry.
- **Factions present.** The Night Post runs the village ([[world/factions/the-night-post]]), and Hushfolk visit the Sorting House to trade with Odile under the Hush Accord ([[world/factions/the-hushfolk]]).

## Design Notes

- **Tutorial by letter.** Every tutorial step is a delivery, because every quest is a letter ([[decisions/product/PDR-P001-letters-are-the-quests|PDR-P001]]).
- **Opening letters is taught, not hidden.** The tutorial shows the open-or-deliver choice on purpose, after 5 of 5 paper-prototype players called it the most discussed moment ([[research/playtests/pt-01-paper-prototype]] [emerging], [[decisions/product/PDR-P008-sealed-letters-can-be-opened|PDR-P008]]).
- **Kept in the three-region cut.** As the hub and tutorial, Thimble Cross was never at risk in [[decisions/product/PDR-P012-three-regions|PDR-P012]].
