# The Drowned Bell

**Cut on 2026-09-08 by [[decisions/product/PDR-P012-three-regions|PDR-P012]]. Kept for reference; do not build.**

The Drowned Bell was a flooded chapel village downriver of Sallow Ferry, planned as the fourth region. Its best story, the Tolley letters, moved to [[world/regions/sallow-ferry]], and the village itself now appears only in letters and in Bram Ashby's stories.

> "Ring till they're all out, Maud. Ring till they're all out."
> — last line of a letter found in the bell tower, in the cut script

## What the Region Was

- **Setting.** A chapel village on low ground beside the river Sallow, flooded sixty-one years before the game. Only the bell tower and a few rooftops stand above the water ([[world/timeline]]).
- **Planned play.** Wren would pole a flat-bottomed boat between rooftops and parley with things that live in the flooded houses ([[concepts/parley]]).
- **Planned letters.** Letters from survivors' families, letters still arriving for the drowned, and the Tolley story about Maud Tolley, the bell-ringer who rang all night so others could find the way out.

## Why It Was Cut

- **Time.** Five regions needed an estimated 22 months against 14 months of runway; three regions needed about 13 ([[research/production/scope-and-burn]] [emerging]).
- **Outside view.** Two of three publishers said five regions was too much for three people by October 2027 ([[research/market/publisher-pitch-feedback]] [emerging]).
- **Words.** Five regions meant 142,000 words of script; three regions meant about 86,000 ([[research/narrative/script-word-count]] [emerging]).
- **Its boat mechanics.** The pole-boat travel was a system no other region used, so cutting the region removed a whole system as well as its content.

## Design Notes

- **Superseded plan.** The region came from the five-region plan in [[decisions/product/PDR-P002-five-regions|PDR-P002]], which PDR-P012 superseded.
- **Constraint behind the cut.** Scope must fit three people and an October 2027 launch ([[architecture/CONSTITUTION]] P4).
