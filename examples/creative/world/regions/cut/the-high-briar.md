# The High Briar

**Cut on 2026-09-08 by [[decisions/product/PDR-P012-three-regions|PDR-P012]]. Kept for reference; do not build.**

The High Briar was a region of thorn uplands north of Gloamwick, planned as the fifth region. Its edge survives as the Briar Edge on Gloamwick's northern side, with the briar hound and a few shepherd letters.

> "The hedge takes a thread from every coat. Count your threads coming home."
> — upland saying, in the cut script

## What the Region Was

- **Setting.** Windy uplands crossed by thorn hedges taller than a house, where shepherds kept sheep in hedge-walled folds.
- **Planned play.** Hedge gaps opened and closed with the moon on a separate schedule from the forest paths, so Wren would track two kinds of shifting at once ([[concepts/moon-shift]]).
- **Planned letters.** Shepherds writing to children who had moved to the marsh, and a long feud between two families over a fold wall.

## Why It Was Cut

- **Time and money.** Five regions needed an estimated 22 months against 14 months of runway ([[research/production/scope-and-burn]] [emerging]).
- **Outside view.** Two of three publishers said five regions was too much for the team ([[research/market/publisher-pitch-feedback]] [emerging]).
- **Lost players.** Playtest 4 showed 6 of 10 players getting lost after the first moon-shift ([[research/playtests/pt-04-vertical-slice]] [emerging]). A second shifting system on top of the paths would have made that worse.

## What Moved Where

| Material | New home |
|---|---|
| Thorn hedges and upland path art | The Briar Edge in [[world/regions/gloamwick]] |
| The briar hound | The Briar Edge, as a [[concepts/parley]] creature ([[world/hollowmere]]) |
| Shepherd letters | Trimmed to a few side letters in Gloamwick |
| Fold-wall feud | Dropped |
| Hedge gaps on their own moon schedule | Dropped |

## Design Notes

- **Superseded plan.** The region came from the five-region plan in [[decisions/product/PDR-P002-five-regions|PDR-P002]].
- **Constraint behind the cut.** Scope must fit three people and an October 2027 launch ([[architecture/CONSTITUTION]] P4).
