# Sallow Ferry

Sallow Ferry is a river crossing run by the Ashby family, where smugglers' letters move oil past the Union and an old debt between the Ashbys and the Tolleys waits to be settled. It holds the story moved from the cut Drowned Bell.

> "Fare's a coin going over. Coming back, it's whatever you've got."
> — Bram Ashby

## What the Player Does Here

- **Rides the ferry.** Bram Ashby takes Wren across the river Sallow, and the fare depends on what Wren knows about him ([[world/characters/bram-ashby]]).
- **Carries smugglers' letters.** Some letters look like love letters but carry oil shipments in a simple code. Wren can deliver them, read them, or hand what they say to the Union ([[world/factions/lamplighters-union]]).
- **Settles the Tolley debt.** The region's main story runs through letters between Bram and the Tolley family about the night the Drowned Bell flooded.

## Places

- **The Ferry House.** The Ashby home and toll booth on the near bank, with sixty years of fare ledgers in the attic.
- **The Landing.** The far-bank jetty where smugglers' crates wait under tarpaulin.
- **The Downriver View.** A bend in the river path where, on clear nights, the top of the Drowned Bell's tower shows above the water.

## The Tolley Letters

This story moved here from [[world/regions/cut/the-drowned-bell]] when that region was cut.

- **The grievance.** For sixty-one years the Tolleys have believed Amos Ashby refused to row back for Maud Tolley, the bell-ringer who drowned ringing the chapel bell ([[world/timeline]]).
- **The letters.** June Tolley writes to Bram from Gloamwick asking for an apology his family has never given. Bram writes back but never sends the letters, and Wren finds them in the Ferry House.
- **What opening them reveals.** Amos's fare ledger shows he did go back, and the river lantern that would have guided him was dark because of the oil dispute. The debt runs through the Union and the Post as much as through the Ashbys.
- **How it ends.** Wren chooses which letters to carry and what to tell each side. No ending names a villain; each one changes who speaks to whom in the final nights.

## Connections

- **North to Thimble Cross** by the river road ([[world/regions/thimble-cross]]).
- **North-east to Gloamwick** by the reed path, where June Tolley lives ([[world/regions/gloamwick]]).
- **Downriver to the Drowned Bell**, which appears only in letters and stories since the cut.

## Design Notes

- **Moved story from a cut region.** [[decisions/product/PDR-P012-three-regions|PDR-P012]] cut the Drowned Bell on 2026-09-08 and moved its best story here, so the region carries both the smugglers' letters and the Tolley letters.
- **Opening letters drives the plot.** The Tolley story only resolves fully if Wren reads letters not meant for Wren, which is the consequence model in [[decisions/product/PDR-P008-sealed-letters-can-be-opened|PDR-P008]] and [[concepts/sealed-letter]].
- **Word budget.** Two stories in one region means more letters here than anywhere else, and letters are 58% of the script's words ([[research/narrative/script-word-count]] [emerging]). Each Sallow Ferry letter stays inside the [[concepts/letter-budget]].
