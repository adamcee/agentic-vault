# Postmark Badges

Reward the way a player delivers with collectable badges in a stamp book: one for a fast delivery, one for a careful one, one for a kind one, and one for a nosy one. The badges would be cosmetic, a record of play style.

## Why It Came Up

The letter-opening choice was the most discussed moment for all 5 paper prototype players ([[research/playtests/pt-01-paper-prototype]] [emerging]). Ines wanted the game to notice how a player delivers, not only what, and it had no progression system yet.

## What Happened

Absorbed on 2026-05-27 into [[concepts/postmark]]. The four badges became four kinds of postmark, and instead of staying cosmetic they unlock abilities, which made them the game's progression system ([[architecture/systems/postmarks]]).
