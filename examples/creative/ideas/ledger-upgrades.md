# Ledger Upgrades

Let Ledger, the retired postal crow, regain old skills as the game goes on: scouting further ahead, spotting Hushfolk, or carrying one light letter on his own. Wren would earn these through postmarks or small favours to Ledger.

## Why It Came Up

In the vertical slice playtest, 6 of 10 players got lost after the first moon-shift ([[research/playtests/pt-04-vertical-slice]] [emerging]). Fixed landmarks became the main fix, and Tomas suggested Ledger, the game's only companion, could take on more of the job in later nights ([[world/characters/ledger]]).

## Open Questions

- **Overlap with postmarks.** Postmarks already unlock abilities, so Ledger's upgrades might belong inside that system ([[concepts/postmark]], [[architecture/systems/postmarks]]).
- **Navigation balance.** Better scouting makes the forest easier to learn, which could undercut the [[concepts/moon-shift]] mastery the same way a full map would ([[ideas/foldable-map]]).
- **Carrying letters.** A crow that delivers letters cuts into Wren's core job ([[decisions/product/PDR-P001-letters-are-the-quests|PDR-P001]]).
