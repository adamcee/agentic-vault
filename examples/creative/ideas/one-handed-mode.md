# One-Handed Mode

Add a control scheme that plays the whole game with one hand on a handheld PC or a controller. Walking, parley choices, the letter reader, and satchel management would all map to one side of the device.

## Why It Came Up

The letter readability audit put accessibility on the agenda ([[research/accessibility/letter-readability-audit]] [emerging]). The Game Accessibility Guidelines summarized in [[research/accessibility/text-contrast-and-size-standards]] [established] cover text, and Ines wanted to check controls the same way.

## Open Questions

- **Audience.** 61% of survey respondents play on a handheld PC at least sometimes, where two-handed reach matters most ([[research/market/community-survey-01]] [emerging], [[concepts/handheld-floor]]).
- **Fit.** Night Post has no timed combat after [[decisions/product/PDR-P009-parley-encounters|PDR-P009]], so most inputs can wait for the player.
- **Cost.** Remapping touches every screen, including the [[architecture/ui/letter-reader]] and [[concepts/satchel-weight]] management.
