# Layered Hints Written into Ines's Journal

What if a stuck player could open the journal and find Ines's own notes, one line at a time, moving from what the clue is to what it means? The help would stay in Ines's voice, with no hint button.

Round 2 players who quit had found the tide-chart clue but did not see what it meant ([[research/playtest-round-2]] [emerging]). Adopting this would supersede [[decisions/product/PDR-P003-no-hint-system|PDR-P003]], so it waits for playtest round 3.
