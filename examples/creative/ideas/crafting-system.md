# Crafting System

Let Wren gather forest materials on the road, such as tallow-reed, glow-moss, and feathers, and craft them into lantern oil, parley offers, and satchel upgrades.

## Why It Came Up

Ines sketched the night economy in the first week, and crafting looked like a way to give walks between deliveries something to do ([[concepts/lantern-oil]], [[concepts/satchel-weight]]).

## What Happened

- **Rejected on 2026-04-21.** Crafting adds inventory busywork that fights the delivery loop. Every stop to gather is a stop that isn't a delivery, and a satchel full of materials crowds out letters.
- **What replaced it.** Oil is bought from the Lamplighters' Union, and offers are bought or given in villages ([[world/factions/lamplighters-union]], [[architecture/systems/lantern-oil-economy]]).
- **Scope.** Dropping it also kept the game inside what three people can build ([[architecture/CONSTITUTION]] P4).
