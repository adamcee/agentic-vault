# Waymark Landmarks

Give every waymark a fixed landmark that looks the same under every moon, such as a split oak, a boot nailed to the post, or a painted milestone. Paths would still move with the moon, but players would always know which waymark they were standing at.

## Why It Came Up

In the vertical slice playtest, 6 of 10 players got lost after the first moon-shift ([[research/playtests/pt-04-vertical-slice]] [emerging]). Players could not tell one waymark from another once the paths changed.

## What Happened

- **Absorbed on 2026-08-18.** The landmarks became a task in [[prototype/implementation-plan]]. They needed no new decision record, because they change art and level layout, not the rule that paths move.
- **The decision stood.** [[decisions/product/PDR-P004-moon-shifting-paths|PDR-P004]] was weakened by playtest 4 the same day, not superseded.
- **It worked.** In playtest 5, 2 of 12 players got lost, down from 6 of 10 ([[research/playtests/pt-05-demo-build]] [emerging]).
- **Defined.** See [[concepts/waymark]] and [[concepts/moon-shift]].
