# Talk Instead of Fight

Replace turn-based encounters with a conversation: Wren gets past a creature by talking, offering something from the satchel, or shining the lantern. Nobody loses health and nobody dies.

## Why It Came Up

- **Paper prototype doubt.** 3 of 5 players said the paper "combat" felt like a different game ([[research/playtests/pt-01-paper-prototype]] [emerging]).
- **Greybox confirmed it.** Turn-based encounters averaged 4 minutes, 5 of 6 players said they clashed with the tone, and 4 of 6 walked around creatures to avoid them ([[research/playtests/pt-02-greybox-encounters]] [emerging]).
- **The plan at the time.** Encounters followed [[decisions/product/PDR-P003-turn-based-encounters|PDR-P003]].

## What Happened

- **Prototyped.** Tomas built a parley prototype, and 7 of 8 players preferred it to the earlier encounters ([[research/playtests/pt-03-parley-prototype]] [supported]).
- **Promoted on 2026-06-24.** [[decisions/product/PDR-P009-parley-encounters|PDR-P009]] adopted parley and superseded PDR-P003. The same day added P1, no death and no fail state from encounters, to [[architecture/CONSTITUTION]].
- **Defined.** The term now lives in [[concepts/parley]].
