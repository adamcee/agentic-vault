# Release Plan
*Updated 2026-10-09 after the demo festival.*

Night Post launches in October 2027 on PC with handheld PC support, at $18, in English, Spanish, and German. Production runs from 2026-10-07 to content lock in May 2027, and localization and beta testing fill the summer.

## Launch Terms
- **Date:** October 2027, held by clause P4 of [[architecture/CONSTITUTION]].
- **Price:** $18 ([[decisions/product/PDR-P010-price-18-dollars|PDR-P010]]), with no launch discount planned.
- **Languages:** English, Spanish, and German at launch; other languages after launch ([[decisions/product/PDR-P014-launch-languages|PDR-P014]]).
- **Voice:** voiced barks only, meaning short spoken reactions with no voiced dialogue ([[decisions/rejected/PDR-P013-full-voice-acting|PDR-P013]]).
- **Content:** three regions, about 86,000 words of script ([[decisions/product/PDR-P012-three-regions|PDR-P012]], [[research/narrative/script-word-count]] [emerging]).

## Marketing Beats
- **Devlog newsletter:** one issue a month, the channel that produced the 214 survey responses ([[research/market/community-survey-01]] [emerging]).
- **Second demo festival:** enter one more online demo festival in spring 2027 with a Gloamwick demo. The first festival raised wishlists from 1,900 to 6,450 ([[research/market/demo-festival-results]] [emerging]).
- **Store page:** add festival comments about the letters and tone to the page before the end of 2026.
- **Launch trailer:** Priya cuts it from the release candidate, and Tomas scores it from the game's music layers ([[decisions/technical/ADR-T006-adaptive-music-layers|ADR-T006]]).

## Risks to the Date
- **Runway:** three regions need about 13 months against 14 months of runway, which leaves one month of slack ([[research/production/scope-and-burn]] [emerging]). [[strategy/funding]] covers the response.
- **Localization cost:** each language costs about $9,500 to $13,800 at 86,000 words ([[research/localization/localization-cost-estimate]] [emerging]). A vendor is not chosen yet.
- **Map requests:** 23 festival comments asked for a map ([[research/market/demo-festival-results]] [emerging]). Adding one would cost art and design time that the milestones above do not include ([[ideas/foldable-map]]).

Tasks for each milestone live in [[prototype/implementation-plan]].
