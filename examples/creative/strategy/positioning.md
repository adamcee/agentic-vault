# Positioning
*Updated 2026-10-09 with festival results.*

Night Post is the narrative adventure where you carry other people's letters through a forest that moves, and you can read them. It sits at the short, cheaper end of its genre, and it has no combat.

## One-Line Pitch
You are the newest night courier in a forest whose paths change with the moon, and every letter you carry is a quest you can open.

## How Night Post Compares
The eight comparable narrative adventures in [[research/market/genre-comparables]] (Game A to Game H) run 5 to 12 hours and launched at $15 to $25, with a median of $19.99 [emerging]. The $18 price is set in [[decisions/product/PDR-P010-price-18-dollars|PDR-P010]].

| | Comparables (Game A–H) | Night Post |
|---|---|---|
| Length | 5–12 hours | 8–10 hours |
| Launch price | $15–$25, median $19.99 | $18 |
| Conflict | Varies | Parley only, no death |
| Platforms | PC | PC and handheld PC |

## What Sets Night Post Apart
- **Letters are the quests.** Every task in the game is a letter to deliver ([[decisions/product/PDR-P001-letters-are-the-quests|PDR-P001]]). Players can open sealed letters, and the consequences are social ([[decisions/product/PDR-P008-sealed-letters-can-be-opened|PDR-P008]]).
- **The forest moves.** Paths between [[concepts/waymark|waymarks]] rearrange by moon phase, so learning [[world/hollowmere]] is the skill the game teaches ([[decisions/product/PDR-P004-moon-shifting-paths|PDR-P004]]).
- **Nobody is the villain.** Stories turn on unpaid debts and old grudges between villages, told one letter at a time.
- **It plays well on a handheld.** The game holds 30 frames per second at 800p on the handheld floor, and every letter is readable at that size (clauses S2 and P2 of [[architecture/CONSTITUTION]]).

## Evidence That the Pitch Works
- Festival comments praised the letters and the tone ([[research/market/demo-festival-results]] [emerging]).
- Wishlists rose from 1,900 to 6,450 during the festival week ([[research/market/demo-festival-results]] [emerging]).
- 23 festival comments asked for a map, which tests the moving-forest pitch. The question is open in [[ideas/foldable-map]].

## Messages to Avoid
- "Cozy" alone undersells the eerie half of the tone. Store copy pairs it with the night and the moving forest.
- Comparisons to named games stay out of public copy until a publisher or the team chooses them on purpose.
