# Goals and Audience
*Updated 2026-10-09 with festival results.*

Night Post is for players who want a short, gentle story game they can finish in a few evenings, often on a handheld PC. The goal is to ship an 8 to 10 hour game at $18 in October 2027, made by three people without running out of money.

## Audience Is Story-First and Often Handheld
- **Core players:** adults who play narrative adventures for characters and mood rather than challenge. They want to finish a game, and they dislike combat that pulls them out of a story ([[research/playtests/pt-02-greybox-encounters]] [emerging]).
- **Handheld players:** 61% of surveyed followers play on a handheld PC at least sometimes ([[research/market/community-survey-01]] [emerging]). The handheld PC is the minimum target in [[decisions/technical/ADR-T002-handheld-performance-floor|ADR-T002]] and the reading size in clause P2 of [[architecture/CONSTITUTION]].
- **Price-aware buyers:** 44% of surveyed followers said they would buy at $18 or less ([[research/market/community-survey-01]] [emerging]).
- **Spanish and German readers:** these were the two most requested languages after English ([[research/market/community-survey-01]] [emerging]), so the game launches in both ([[decisions/product/PDR-P014-launch-languages|PDR-P014]]).

## Goals for Launch
- **Ship on time:** launch in October 2027 with three regions, as set by clause P4 of [[architecture/CONSTITUTION]] and [[decisions/product/PDR-P012-three-regions|PDR-P012]].
- **Stay inside runway:** finish within the 14 months of savings counted on 2026-09-05 ([[research/production/scope-and-burn]] [emerging]). The plan is in [[strategy/funding]].
- **Grow wishlists:** reach 20,000 store wishlists by launch, up from 6,450 after the demo festival ([[research/market/demo-festival-results]] [emerging]).
- **Keep letters readable:** every letter passes clause P2 at handheld size in all three launch languages.

## What Success Looks Like
- Players finish the game. The demo finish rate of 41% is the baseline to beat in later builds ([[research/market/demo-festival-results]] [emerging]).
- Reviews and comments name the letters and the tone, as festival comments already did.
- Players argue about which letters they opened, which is the moment playtesters discussed most ([[research/playtests/pt-01-paper-prototype]] [emerging]).

## Out of Scope
- Combat, health, and death, as set by clause P1 of [[architecture/CONSTITUTION]] and [[decisions/product/PDR-P009-parley-encounters|PDR-P009]].
- Online play and microtransactions, as set by clause P3 and [[decisions/rejected/PDR-P007-co-op-courier-mode|PDR-P007]].
- Full voice acting ([[decisions/rejected/PDR-P013-full-voice-acting|PDR-P013]]).
- Consoles before launch. The team can port later if sales justify it.

Positioning against similar games is in [[strategy/positioning]], and the dated launch plan is in [[strategy/release-plan]].
