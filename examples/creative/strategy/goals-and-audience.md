# Low Tide Goals and Audience

Low Tide aims to be a finished, two-to-three-hour narrative puzzle game that two people ship to PC without crunch. Finishing well matters more than size.

## Audience
- **Primary.** Adults who play short story-driven games in one or two evenings and like puzzles that reward close looking.
- **Secondary.** Players who follow small studios and buy on art style from store screenshots [unsourced].

## Goals
- **Ship a complete game.** Every scene painted, written, and tested, with no cut-short final act.
- **Keep scope inside two people.** Nothing on the plan needs a third person ([[architecture/CONSTITUTION]] P1).
- **Make the tide the core.** Each puzzle uses the [[concepts/tide-window]].

## What Success Looks Like
- **Finish rate.** At least 7 of 8 playtesters finish each build without outside help. Round 2 reached 6 of 8 ([[research/playtest-round-2]] [emerging]).
- **Play time.** First-run play time lands between two and three hours.
- **Release.** A PC build on Windows, macOS, and Linux.
