# Funding
*Updated 2026-10-09 after the demo festival.*

Night Post is self-funded from the team's savings, with 14 months of runway counted on 2026-09-05. That covers the three-region plan with one month to spare, so outside money would buy safety and localization, not scope.

## Runway Covers Three Regions, Not Five
- **Runway:** 14 months at current spend, counted on 2026-09-05 ([[research/production/scope-and-burn]] [emerging]).
- **Five regions:** an estimated 22 months of work, which is 8 months past the money ([[research/production/scope-and-burn]] [emerging]).
- **Three regions:** about 13 months of work ([[research/production/scope-and-burn]] [emerging]). This gap drove [[decisions/product/PDR-P012-three-regions|PDR-P012]].

## Costs Outside Current Spend
- **Localization:** Spanish and German together cost about $18,900 to $27,500 at 86,000 words per language and $0.11 to $0.16 per word ([[research/localization/localization-cost-estimate]] [emerging]).
- **Voiced barks:** a small recording budget for short spoken reactions, chosen over full voice acting to keep this line small ([[decisions/rejected/PDR-P013-full-voice-acting|PDR-P013]]).

## Northern Arts Fund Grant
- **What it is:** a regional arts grant for creative projects by local artists.
- **What the team asks for:** the localization cost, so the launch languages in [[decisions/product/PDR-P014-launch-languages|PDR-P014]] do not draw on runway.
- **Status:** the application is in progress, due 2026-11-30. The festival numbers go in as evidence of an audience ([[research/market/demo-festival-results]] [emerging]).

## Publisher Talks
- **First round:** the team pitched three unnamed publishers between 2026-08-24 and 2026-09-01. Two of the three said five regions was too much for three people by October 2027, and one offered a follow-up after the demo ([[research/market/publisher-pitch-feedback]] [emerging]).
- **Next step:** send that publisher the festival results and the three-region plan.
- **Terms to protect:** the team keeps the October 2027 date, the $18 price, and the offline, single-player design under clause P3 of [[architecture/CONSTITUTION]]. A deal that requires online features or microtransactions is a no.

## If Money Runs Short
- **First cut:** drop the second demo festival in [[strategy/release-plan]] and keep the date.
- **Second cut:** launch in English only and add Spanish and German in a free update. That change needs a new PDR to supersede [[decisions/product/PDR-P014-launch-languages|PDR-P014]].
