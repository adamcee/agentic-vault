# Example Vault: Low Tide

Low Tide is a fictional short narrative puzzle game for PC, made by a two-person team (Mara, a writer-designer, and Teo, an artist-programmer), and this vault shows their project about six weeks in. Two playtests and a scope estimate turned its branching story into a linear one, and the second playtest put the no-hints decision under review.

## Tour

1. **Master file:** [AGENTS.md](AGENTS.md) holds the project, current phase, standing decisions, and Last Session Checkpoint.
2. **Invariants:** [architecture/CONSTITUTION.md](architecture/CONSTITUTION.md) lists five rules, such as two-person scope and offline play.
3. **Superseded decision:** [PDR-P001](decisions/product/PDR-P001-branching-three-endings.md) (branching story) was replaced by [PDR-P005](decisions/product/PDR-P005-linear-story-local-choices.md) (linear story), which explains what changed.
4. **Rejected decision:** [PDR-P004](decisions/rejected/PDR-P004-online-co-op.md) records why online co-op was declined.
5. **A reweave:** the Reweave Triggers in [playtest round 2](research/playtest-round-2.md) led to the dated "weakened" note in [PDR-P003](decisions/product/PDR-P003-no-hint-system.md) and new tasks in the [production plan](prototype/implementation-plan.md).
