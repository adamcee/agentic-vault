# The Hushfolk — An Expanded Lore Compendium

## A Note Before We Begin

What follows is an expansive and richly detailed exploration of the Hushfolk, one of the most enigmatic and captivating factions in all of Hollowmere. In crafting this compendium, I have sought to weave together existing threads of lore with new and evocative details that could help deepen the player's sense of wonder and mystery. It is worth noting that some of these ideas are speculative, and the team should feel free to adapt them as they see fit.

## Origins: Born of Moonlight and Silence

The Hushfolk are an ancient, mysterious, and otherworldly people whose origins are shrouded in the mists of time. According to legend, they dwelt among the hollow oaks long before the first village was ever built, born, some say, from the silence between the moon's phases. Some say they are the forest's memory given wings; others whisper that they are the forest's conscience. In truth, perhaps they are both, and perhaps they are neither.

Moth-like in appearance, the Hushfolk possess delicate, dust-soft grey wings that they fold about themselves like a cloak, shimmering faintly in lantern light. Their eyes are large, dark, and impossibly deep, and many travelers report feeling as though a Hushfolk can see straight into their soul.

## The Art of Beautiful Deception

Perhaps the most fascinating and defining aspect of Hushfolk culture is their mastery of deception. The Hushfolk are consummate liars, weaving intricate webs of falsehood with breathtaking skill and subtlety. To a Hushfolk, a lie is not a moral failing but an art form, a gift to be crafted with care and offered to those worthy of receiving it. A courier who trusts the word of a Hushfolk without question does so at their own peril, for the moth-folk delight in leading travelers astray with half-truths and elegant fabrications.

This tradition of deception gives rise to a rich and nuanced gameplay opportunity: players could be required to carefully weigh every Hushfolk statement, cross-referencing it against letters they have read, in order to separate truth from fiction. This would add a robust layer of strategic depth to the parley system.

## The Secret Economy

The Hushfolk trade in secrets the way the villages trade in coin. A secret freely given is worth little; a secret pried loose from a sealed letter is worth a great deal. Over the centuries, the Hushfolk have built a vast and intricate tapestry of knowledge, a hidden archive of every confession, debt, and broken promise ever whispered beneath Hollowmere's canopy.

## Mothwife Sel: The Silent Matriarch

At the heart of Hushfolk society sits Mothwife Sel, the reclusive and ancient matriarch who rules her people from the deepest hollow of the forest. Mothwife Sel never leaves the deep wood and never speaks to outsiders; she has not been seen by a human in over a hundred years. Instead, she communicates only through a small circle of trusted attendants, who carry her will to the farthest corners of Hollowmere. Couriers of the Night Post speak of her in hushed tones, and some doubt she exists at all.

Her silence is a testament to her power. In a culture built on the exchange of secrets, the one who reveals nothing holds everything.

## Relationships With Other Factions

The Hushfolk maintain a complex and multifaceted relationship with the other factions of Hollowmere. They view the Night Post with a mixture of curiosity and amusement, seeing the couriers as useful but naive carriers of the very secrets the Hushfolk prize. Their relationship with the Lamplighters' Union is more distant, a careful and courteous arrangement in which each side keeps a respectful remove from the other.

## Potential Future Directions

There is significant potential to further expand Hushfolk lore in future content. Possible directions include a Hushfolk homeland region, a playable Hushfolk character, a Hushfolk language with its own script, seasonal Hushfolk festivals, and a branching questline in which Wren must decide whether to join the Hushfolk or remain loyal to the Night Post. Each of these could add tremendous depth and replayability to the overall experience, seamlessly enriching the world of Night Post.
