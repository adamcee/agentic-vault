# Postmark

A postmark is a stamp the Night Post gives Wren for how a letter was delivered, and postmarks are the game's progression system. Collecting postmarks of a kind unlocks abilities that suit that style of delivery ([[architecture/systems/postmarks]]).

## How It Works in Play

- **Four kinds.** Postmarks come in fast, careful, kind, and nosy.
- **Earned at dawn.** Odile Marr stamps each delivery when Wren reports back at the Sorting House ([[world/characters/odile-marr]]).
- **Style, not score.** A nosy postmark comes from opening a [[concepts/sealed-letter]], and a kind one from helping someone on the way. No kind is better than another.
- **Abilities.** Each kind unlocks its own abilities, listed in the system doc.

## Where It Came From

[[ideas/postmark-badges]] proposed collectable badges for delivery style. Postmarks absorbed that idea and turned the badges into the progression system.
