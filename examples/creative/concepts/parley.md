# Parley

Parley is how Night Post resolves every encounter: Wren talks, offers an item from the satchel, or shines the lantern until the creature or person lets Wren pass. There is no health, no damage, and no death ([[decisions/product/PDR-P009-parley-encounters|PDR-P009]]).

## How It Works in Play

- **Talk.** Wren chooses what to say, and what Wren has read in opened letters can open new lines. Hushfolk accept secrets as payment ([[world/factions/the-hushfolk]]).
- **Offer.** Wren gives an item from the satchel. Carrying offers costs [[concepts/satchel-weight]].
- **Shine.** Wren raises the lantern, which spends [[concepts/lantern-oil]]. Some creatures, like the Tallow Stag, respond only to light ([[world/hollowmere]]).
- **Failing costs time, not life.** A parley that goes badly sends Wren the long way round or costs oil, never a game over ([[architecture/CONSTITUTION]] P1).

The full rules live in [[architecture/systems/parley]].

## Where It Came From

- **Turn-based encounters failed.** They averaged 4 minutes, 5 of 6 players said they clashed with the tone, and 4 of 6 walked around creatures to avoid them ([[research/playtests/pt-02-greybox-encounters]] [emerging]). That superseded [[decisions/product/PDR-P003-turn-based-encounters|PDR-P003]].
- **Parley tested better.** 7 of 8 players preferred parley to the earlier encounters ([[research/playtests/pt-03-parley-prototype]] [supported]).
- **The idea note.** [[ideas/talk-instead-of-fight]] proposed it after playtest 2.
