# Waymark

A waymark is a lantern post where paths meet in Hollowmere, and it is the one point on a route that never moves. Paths rearrange between waymarks with each [[concepts/moon-shift]], so players navigate from waymark to waymark ([[architecture/systems/moon-and-paths]]).

## How It Works in Play

- **Fixed points.** Wren plans each night's route as a chain of waymarks.
- **Landmarks.** Each waymark has a fixed landmark, such as a split oak or a boot nailed to the post, so players can recognise it under any moon ([[ideas/waymark-landmarks]]).
- **Lit by the Union.** The Lamplighters' Union lights waymark lanterns at dusk ([[world/factions/lamplighters-union]]).
- **Route lanterns.** Red and green route lanterns at waymarks also differ in shape, so colourblind players can tell them apart ([[decisions/product/PDR-P005-painted-night-palette|PDR-P005]], [[research/accessibility/night-palette-colorblind-test]] [emerging]).

## Decisions Behind It

- **Landmarks came from playtest 4.** 6 of 10 players got lost after the first moon-shift, which weakened [[decisions/product/PDR-P004-moon-shifting-paths|PDR-P004]] and added the landmark task to [[prototype/implementation-plan]] ([[research/playtests/pt-04-vertical-slice]] [emerging]).
- **The fix worked.** In playtest 5, 2 of 12 players got lost ([[research/playtests/pt-05-demo-build]] [emerging]).
