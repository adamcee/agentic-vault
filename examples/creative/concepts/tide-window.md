# Tide Window

A tide window is the stretch of play during one low tide when the seabed around the island is walkable. Each puzzle in Low Tide must be solved inside one window.

- **Shown in the scene.** The waterline and the palette tint show how much time is left; there is no on-screen timer ([[decisions/product/PDR-P002-hand-painted-limited-palette|PDR-P002]]).
- **Failure resets the puzzle.** If the tide returns, the puzzle resets and the save stays intact ([[architecture/CONSTITUTION]] P2).
- **Hints are the open question.** A stuck player watching the water return is why hints are under review ([[decisions/product/PDR-P003-no-hint-system|PDR-P003]]).
