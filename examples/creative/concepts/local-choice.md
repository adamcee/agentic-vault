# Local Choice

A local choice is a player decision whose effects stay inside the current scene or the next one and never change the ending. Reading or burning the keeper's letter in act 2 is the model case.

- **What it changes.** Lines of dialogue, an object in a later scene, or what Ines writes in her journal.
- **What it never changes.** The route, the ending, or which puzzles the player meets.
- **Why it exists.** It keeps choices meaningful within a linear story ([[decisions/product/PDR-P005-linear-story-local-choices|PDR-P005]]).
