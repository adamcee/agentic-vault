# Lantern Oil

Lantern oil is both the night's clock and its currency: Wren's lantern burns it while walking, and Wren spends it to buy, bribe, and parley. When the oil runs low, the night is nearly over for Wren ([[architecture/systems/lantern-oil-economy]]).

## How It Works in Play

- **Clock.** The lantern burns oil as Wren travels, so a long route costs more oil than a short one.
- **Currency.** Villagers accept oil as payment, and the Lamplighters' Union sells it in Gloamwick ([[world/factions/lamplighters-union]]).
- **Parley tool.** Shining the lantern during a [[concepts/parley]] spends oil.
- **Weight trade-off.** Spare oil adds [[concepts/satchel-weight]].

## In the World

Oil comes from tallow-reed rendered in Gloamwick, and the price of oil is the oldest quarrel in Hollowmere ([[world/hollowmere]], [[world/timeline]]). The Night Post has refused the Union's bill for sixty-one years ([[world/factions/the-night-post]]).
