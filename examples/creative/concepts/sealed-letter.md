# Sealed Letter

A sealed letter is a letter Wren carries but is not meant to read, and Wren may open it anyway. What Wren reads changes what people say to Wren and what happens next, but the consequences are always social, never a game over ([[decisions/product/PDR-P008-sealed-letters-can-be-opened|PDR-P008]]).

## How It Works in Play

- **The choice.** At any point before delivery, Wren can break the seal and read the letter in the [[architecture/ui/letter-reader]].
- **People notice.** A recipient may spot a broken seal, and characters react to what Wren now knows.
- **Knowledge has uses.** What Wren learns can open new lines in a [[concepts/parley]] or be sold to the Hushfolk ([[world/characters/mothwife-sel]]).
- **The Post keeps score.** Opening letters earns the nosy [[concepts/postmark]].

## Evidence Behind It

- **The most discussed moment.** In the paper prototype, all 5 players named the letter-opening choice as the moment they talked about most ([[research/playtests/pt-01-paper-prototype]] [emerging]).
- **Held up in the parley prototype.** Playtest 3 strengthened the decision ([[research/playtests/pt-03-parley-prototype]] [supported]).
- **Core fantasy.** Every quest is a letter, so the choice to read one is the main moral choice of the game ([[decisions/product/PDR-P001-letters-are-the-quests|PDR-P001]]).
