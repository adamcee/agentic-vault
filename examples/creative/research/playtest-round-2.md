# Playtest Round 2: Local Choices Landed, but the Tide-Chart Puzzle Stopped Players

Players liked the linear story's local choices, and the low replay intent from round 1 held. The act 2 tide-chart puzzle stalled most players, which weakens the no-hints decision.

## Method
- **Build.** The linear vertical slice: act 1 rewritten for [[decisions/product/PDR-P005-linear-story-local-choices|PDR-P005]] plus the first half of act 2, about 50 minutes.
- **Players.** Eight adults: three returning from [[research/playtest-round-1]] and five new.
- **Format.** Same as round 1, with one added question about being stuck.

## Findings and Their Maturity
- **Low replay intent, `[supported]`.** 7 of 8 would not replay. This repeats round 1 across a different build and mostly new players.
- **Local choices liked, `[emerging]`.** 6 of 8 named the choice to read or burn the keeper's letter as a highlight. None asked about other endings.
- **Tide-chart puzzle stalls, `[emerging]`.** 5 of 8 were stuck for over 10 minutes. Two quit there, and both said they had found the clue but did not see what it meant.
- **Finish rate.** 6 of 8 finished the slice without help, short of the 7 of 8 target in [[strategy/goals-and-audience]].

## Interpretation
We read the stall to mean the clue is findable but not readable, so a nudge toward meaning may help more than a new clue. One round is not enough to decide between redesigning the puzzle and adding hints.

## Reweave Triggers
- [[decisions/product/PDR-P005-linear-story-local-choices]]: strengthened. Replay intent stayed low, and local choices worked.
- [[decisions/product/PDR-P003-no-hint-system]]: weakened. The no-hints design left most players stuck at one puzzle; flagged for review, not superseded.
- [[strategy/goals-and-audience]]: weakened. The finish-rate target was missed by one player.
- [[prototype/implementation-plan]]: add tasks to prototype layered hints and test them in round 3.
