## Background and Context

As previously discussed, the team has expressed interest in launching Night Post in languages beyond English. Localization represents a multifaceted undertaking that touches virtually every aspect of a narrative-driven title, from letters and dialogue to menus, tutorials, and even the subtle nuances of tone that make the game's world feel alive. Given that Night Post is a word-heavy narrative experience, the cost of translation is arguably one of the most significant line items the team will face, and careful planning is therefore essential.

By leveraging this well-documented effect, the team could potentially recoup the full cost of localization within the first few months after launch, creating a synergistic cycle in which localization revenue funds further localization.
