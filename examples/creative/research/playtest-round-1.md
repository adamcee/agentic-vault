# Playtest Round 1: Players Liked the Puzzles but Would Not Replay for Endings

Five of six playtesters said they would not replay Low Tide to see another ending. This is the team's own first round with a small sample, so every finding here is `[emerging]`.

## Method
- **Build.** Act 1 of the branching script, about 25 minutes, ending at the first branch point.
- **Players.** Six adults who play narrative or puzzle games, recruited from friends of friends. None had seen the game before.
- **Format.** Each played alone while Mara took notes, then answered five questions about story, puzzles, art, and replay.

## Findings
- **Low replay intent.** 5 of 6 said they would not replay to see a different ending. Four said a two-to-three-hour game feels "done" after one run.
- **Branch choice unclear.** 4 of 6 could not say afterward which choice had sent them down their route.
- **Choices themselves liked.** 5 of 6 enjoyed deciding what to do with the logbook pages they found.
- **Puzzles solved without help.** 4 of 6 solved both act 1 puzzles without asking Mara for a hint.
- **Art praised.** All 6 commented on the painted backgrounds without being asked.

## Interpretation
We read the replay and branch findings to mean that three endings cost a lot and return little for this game. A small sample cannot settle that, so the scope estimate is the second check.
