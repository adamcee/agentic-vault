# Publisher Pitch Feedback

Two of three publishers said five regions is too much for three people to finish by October 2027, and one offered a follow-up call after the demo festival. Three conversations are a small and interested sample, so the findings are `[emerging]`.

## Method
- **Calls.** The team held three video calls of about 45 minutes, on 2026-08-24, 2026-08-27, and 2026-09-01. The vault calls the publishers Publisher 1, 2, and 3.
- **Pitch.** Ines showed a 10-minute capture of the vertical slice, then walked through the five-region plan, the team, and the October 2027 launch.
- **Notes.** Tomas took notes during each call, and Ines checked them against her own the same day.

## Findings
| Publisher | Comment on scope | Next step |
|---|---|---|
| Publisher 1 | Five regions is too much for three people by October 2027. | None offered. |
| Publisher 2 | Five regions is too much; suggested cutting to three and launching on time. | None offered. |
| Publisher 3 | Did not raise scope; asked about wishlist numbers. | Offered a follow-up call after the demo festival. |

- **Scope was the main concern.** 2/3 said five regions is too much for the team and the date. Publisher 2 said it would rather see three finished regions than five rushed ones.
- **One door stayed open.** Publisher 3 offered a follow-up after the demo and asked to see festival results.
- **Letters drew praise.** All three publishers singled out the sealed-letter choice in the slice capture.

## Limits
- **Three conversations.** Three publishers cannot speak for the market.
- **Publisher incentives.** Publishers favor projects that look safe to finish, which can push them toward smaller scope regardless of the game.
- **Notes, not recordings.** The calls were not recorded, so quotes are paraphrased from notes.
