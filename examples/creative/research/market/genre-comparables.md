# Genre Comparables: Eight Narrative Adventures

Eight comparable narrative adventures run 5 to 12 hours and launched at $15 to $25, with a median launch price of $19.99. One person picked the set and recorded the numbers in one pass, so the findings are `[emerging]`.

## Method
- **Selection.** Ines picked eight narrative adventures released in the last five years by teams of one to six people, with a painted or illustrated 2D look and little or no combat. The vault names them Game A to Game H.
- **Price.** Ines took each game's launch price in US dollars from its store page history.
- **Length.** She took the typical main-story length from player-reported completion times and rounded it to the nearest hour.

## Findings
| Game | Main-story length | Launch price | Combat |
|---|---|---|---|
| A | 6 hours | $15.00 | None |
| B | 5 hours | $16.99 | None |
| C | 8 hours | $17.99 | Light |
| D | 9 hours | $19.99 | None |
| E | 7 hours | $19.99 | None |
| F | 10 hours | $22.00 | Light |
| G | 12 hours | $24.99 | Light |
| H | 11 hours | $25.00 | None |

- **Length.** The games run 5 to 12 hours. Night Post's planned 8 to 10 hours sits in the middle of the set.
- **Price.** Launch prices run $15 to $25, with a median of $19.99.
- **Longer games cost more.** The three games of 10 hours or more launched at $22 or above.

## Limits
- **Hand-picked set.** One person chose what counts as comparable, and a different set could shift the median.
- **Launch price only.** The scan does not show discounts, sales volume, or regional prices.
- **Reported lengths.** Player-reported completion times vary with play style.
