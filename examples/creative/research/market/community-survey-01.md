# Community Survey 1

Most of the 214 people who answered play on a handheld PC at least sometimes (61%), 44% said they would buy Night Post at $18 or less, and Spanish and German were the two most requested languages after English. Respondents chose to answer and already follow the devlog, so every finding is `[emerging]`.

## Method
- **Form.** The form asked eight questions, four of them on devices, game length, price, and language. Q1 to Q3 were required and Q4 was optional; the other four covered devlog feedback and are not reported here.
- **Reach.** The form was linked from the devlog newsletter and pinned in the community server from 2026-06-29 to 2026-07-08.
- **Sample.** The form collected 214 responses, and no incentive was offered.

- **Handhelds are common.** 131 respondents (61%) play on a handheld PC mostly or sometimes.
- **$18 keeps a large share.** 94 respondents (44%) said they would buy at $18 or less. Yes answers fell from 58% at $15 to 44% at $18, then to 21% at $22.
- **Spanish and German lead.** 102 respondents named at least one language, and Spanish (46) and German (38) were the top two.
- **Length fits.** 57% prefer story-driven games of 5 to 10 hours.

## Limits
- **Fans, not the market.** Devlog followers already like the game's pitch and may say yes to prices more readily than strangers.
- **Stated, not paid.** A yes on a form is not a purchase.
- **Optional language question.** Only 102 of 214 answered Q4, so the language counts reflect people who cared enough to answer.
