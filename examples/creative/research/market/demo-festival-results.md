# Demo Festival Results

During the online demo festival, 3,140 people played the Night Post demo, 41% finished it, and wishlists rose from 1,900 to 6,450. Comments praised the letters and tone, and 23 comments asked for a map; one festival week makes these findings `[emerging]`.

## Method
- **Event.** The online demo festival ran from 2026-09-29 to 2026-10-05, and players used the build described in [[prototype/specs/festival-demo]].
- **Numbers.** Ines exported player counts, completions, and wishlists from the festival dashboard on 2026-10-06.
- **Comments.** Ines read all 171 comments posted on the demo page and in the community server during the festival and tagged each one by theme. One comment can carry more than one theme.

## Findings
| Measure | Result |
|---|---|
| Demo players | 3,140 |
| Finished the demo | 1,287 (41%) |
| Wishlists before the festival | 1,900 |
| Wishlists after the festival | 6,450 |

| Comment theme | Comments |
|---|---|
| Praised the letters or the choice to open them | 58 |
| Praised the tone or atmosphere | 47 |
| Praised parley or the lack of combat | 21 |
| Asked for a map | 23 |
| Reported a bug | 16 |
| Mentioned price | 7 |

- **Wishlists more than tripled.** Wishlists rose by 4,550 during the week.
- **Letters and tone drew the most praise.** Letters (58) and tone (47) were the two most common themes.
- **Players want a map.** 23 comments asked for a map, most of them after the first moon-shift.
- **Price drew little pushback.** Seven comments mentioned the $18 price announced in the devlog; five called it fair and two asked for less.

## Limits
- **Festival players are browsers.** People who try demos at a festival may not behave like buyers at launch.
- **Wishlists are not sales.** A wishlist shows interest, not a purchase.
- **Comment bias.** Players who comment are a small, vocal share of the 3,140.
