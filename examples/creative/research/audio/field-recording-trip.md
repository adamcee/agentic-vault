# Field Recording Trip

Two nights at a wetland nature reserve produced 6 hours of audio and 41 usable takes of frogs, wind in reeds, and distant owls. The takes set the sound of Hollowmere's night; as one trip to one site, the result is `[emerging]`.

## Method
- **Site.** Tomas recorded at a wetland nature reserve on the nights of 2026-05-26 and 2026-05-27, from dusk to about 02:00.
- **Gear.** He used a portable stereo recorder with two small omnidirectional microphones and a wind cover.
- **Logging.** Tomas logged every take on site, then graded each one at home on 2026-05-29. A take counted as usable if it ran at least 60 seconds with no traffic, aircraft, or handling noise.

## Findings
| Category | Takes recorded | Usable takes |
|---|---|---|
| Frogs | 31 | 17 |
| Wind in reeds | 24 | 12 |
| Distant owls | 19 | 7 |
| Water and insects | 14 | 5 |
| **Total** | **88** | **41** |

- **Six hours, 41 keepers.** The two nights produced 6 hours of audio and 41 usable takes.
- **Frogs and reeds are the bed.** Frogs (17) and wind in reeds (12) give enough material for an ambient bed in every region.
- **Owls are scarce.** Only 7 owl takes were usable, because most calls were faint or overlapped road noise.

## Limits
- **One site, one season.** All takes come from one wetland in late May; Thimble Cross's village sounds and Sallow Ferry's river need other sources.
- **Grading by one person.** Tomas alone judged which takes were usable.
