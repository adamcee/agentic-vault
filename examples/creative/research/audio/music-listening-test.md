# Music Listening Test

Seven of nine listeners found continuous music tiring after about 20 minutes, and all nine liked the music returning when they arrived somewhere. Nine listeners in one round make this `[emerging]`.

## Method
- **Route.** Listeners walked a 30-minute route in the vertical slice build with four arrivals: two deliveries, one waymark, and the Night Post office.
- **Listeners.** Nine adults (L1–L9) from the devlog newsletter took part in person from 2026-08-03 to 2026-08-06 and wore headphones.
- **Conditions.** Version A played the score continuously. Version B faded the music to ambience after about three minutes of walking and brought it back at each arrival. Five listeners walked A first and four walked B first.
- **Questions.** Listeners said "tired" aloud whenever the music started to wear on them, and Tomas noted the time. After both walks, they answered four questions about each version.

- **Continuous music wears thin.** 7/9 found continuous music tiring, at a median of 20 minutes.
- **Arrivals want music.** 9/9 liked the music returning at arrivals in version B.
- **Silence was not empty.** Six listeners said the frog and reed ambience made the quiet stretches feel like walking at night.

## Listener Quotes
- **L2:** "By the third delivery the loop was in my teeth."
- **L5:** "When the music came back at the office, I actually smiled."
- **L7:** "I didn't mind the music, but the arrivals in B felt like a reward."

## Limits
- **One score.** The test used one draft score; a different piece might tire listeners at a different point.
- **Saying "tired" aloud.** Asking listeners to report fatigue may make them notice it sooner.
- **Short test.** Thirty minutes is shorter than a full night of play.
