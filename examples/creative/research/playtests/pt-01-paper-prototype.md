# Playtest 1: Paper Prototype

All five players named the choice to open a sealed letter as the moment they remembered most, and three of five said the paper combat felt like a different game. One paper session with five players is thin evidence, so every finding here is `[emerging]`.

## Method
- **Materials.** The session used a hand-drawn map of Thimble Cross and Gloamwick, 10 printed letters (two of them sealed), a deck of satchel item cards, and a dice sheet for fights with forest creatures.
- **Players.** Five adults (P1–P5) took part, all friends of friends of the team who had not seen the game. Three play narrative games every week.
- **Session.** The session ran on 2026-04-23. Each player ran one night of deliveries in about 45 minutes, then answered five debrief questions.
- **Roles.** Ines read the characters' lines aloud, Tomas ran the fights, and Priya logged moments and quotes.

## Findings
- **The sealed letter stopped everyone.** All five players paused at the first sealed letter, a tailor's note to a Gloamwick lamplighter. Four opened it, and P2 asked what would happen before deciding not to.
- **It was the most discussed moment.** Asked "Which moment do you remember most?", 5/5 named the letter-opening choice. Three players kept talking about it after the debrief ended.
- **Combat felt like a different game.** 3/5 said the dice fights felt like a different game from the deliveries. Each paper fight took about six minutes of dice and bookkeeping.
- **Ledger landed.** Four players laughed at the crow's comments, and P4 asked whether Ledger could carry letters too.

### Player Quotes
- **P1:** "I read it, and then I had to hand it over like nothing happened. My face was hot."
- **P3:** "The fight with the bog hound was fine, but I forgot I was a postman for ten minutes."
- **P4:** "Can I tell the lamplighter what the letter said? I want to warn her."
- **P5:** "The fighting is a board game stapled onto a story game."

## Limits
- **Paper is slower than software.** Dice bookkeeping inflates fight length, so "different game" may partly reflect the paper format.
- **Friendly players.** Friends of friends may soften criticism.
- **Untested systems.** Paper cannot show moon-shift (paths that rearrange by moon phase), lantern oil pacing, or reading comfort on a screen.
