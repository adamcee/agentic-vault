# Playtest 5: Demo Build

Five of twelve players turned on the font toggle, and only two of twelve got lost, down from six of ten in the vertical slice after fixed waymark landmarks were added. The median session lasted 38 minutes; one remote session is `[emerging]` evidence.

## Method
- **Build.** The build was the festival demo described in [[prototype/specs/festival-demo]], with fixed waymark landmarks and the readable letter typeface plus font toggle from [[decisions/product/PDR-P011-readable-letters|PDR-P011]].
- **Players.** Twelve adults (P1–P12) took part, none of whom had played earlier builds. Seven played on handheld PCs and five on desktops.
- **Sessions.** Sessions ran remotely from 2026-09-21 to 2026-09-24. Players shared their screen on a video call and played as long as they liked.
- **Measures.** Ines used the pt-04 rule for "lost": three minutes without progress or a request for help. Priya logged settings changes.

## Findings
| Measure | pt-04 vertical slice | pt-05 demo build |
|---|---|---|
| Players | 10 | 12 |
| Got lost after a moon-shift | 6 (60%) | 2 (17%) |
| Turned on font toggle | Not in build | 5 (42%) |
| Median session | 71 minutes to finish | 38 minutes |

- **Landmarks fixed most of the getting lost.** 2/12 got lost, against 6/10 in [[research/playtests/pt-04-vertical-slice]]. Eight players named a landmark when asked how they found their way.
- **The font toggle got used.** 5/12 turned it on, four of them on handhelds. No player leaned in to read letter body text.
- **Sessions ran 38 minutes.** The median session lasted 38 minutes, and nine of twelve players finished the demo.

## Player Quotes
- **P4:** "The big stone heron is how I found my way back."
- **P7:** "I switched the font straight away. The fancy writing at the bottom is still nice."
- **P11:** "I got turned around at the marsh, but the heron is there, so I knew it was me, not the map."
- **P12:** "I didn't even look for a setting. The letters were fine."

## Limits
- **Remote viewing.** Screen share hides faces and posture, so Priya could not watch for squinting as in pt-04.
- **Different builds.** The demo and the slice differ in more than landmarks, so the drop in getting lost has more than one possible cause.
- **Demo length.** The 38-minute median reflects the demo's content, not how long players will want to play the full game.

## Reweave Triggers
- [[decisions/product/PDR-P011-readable-letters|PDR-P011]]: strengthened. Five of twelve players turned on the font toggle, and no player leaned in to read body text.
- [[decisions/product/PDR-P004-moon-shifting-paths|PDR-P004]]: strengthened, for the waymark fix. Getting lost fell from six of ten to two of twelve after fixed waymark landmarks were added.
