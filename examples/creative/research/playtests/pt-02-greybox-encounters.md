# Playtest 2: Greybox Encounters

Turn-based encounters averaged four minutes each, five of six players said they clashed with the game's tone, and four of six walked around creatures to avoid fighting them. This is one session with six players, so the findings are `[emerging]`.

## Method
- **Build.** The build was a greybox (untextured blocks) route from Thimble Cross toward Gloamwick with six turn-based creature encounters and three deliveries.
- **Players.** Six adults (P1–P6) took part, recruited from the devlog newsletter. None had played [[research/playtests/pt-01-paper-prototype]].
- **Sessions.** Sessions ran on 2026-05-19 and 2026-05-20. Each player played for 40 minutes while thinking aloud, then answered six interview questions.
- **Measures.** Tomas logged the start and end time of every fight, and Ines noted every creature a player walked around.

## Findings
| Player | Fights entered | Average fight length | Walked around a creature | Said fights clashed with tone |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| P1 | 3 | 3:40 | Yes | Yes |
| P2 | 5 | 4:10 | No | Yes |
| P3 | 1 | 5:20 | Yes | Yes |
| P4 | 4 | 3:50 | No | No |
| P5 | 2 | 4:30 | Yes | Yes |
| P6 | 2 | 3:30 | Yes | Yes |

- **Fights averaged four minutes.** Across 17 fights, the average length was 4 minutes 2 seconds. Players spent more time fighting than delivering in the first 20 minutes.
- **Fights clashed with the tone.** 5/6 said the fights did not fit a game about delivering letters at night.
- **Players dodged.** 4/6 walked around at least one creature to avoid a fight, and P3 avoided every fight after the first.

### Player Quotes
- **P3:** "I'm a mail carrier. Why am I stabbing a hare?"
- **P5:** "I'd rather walk around it and keep the letter dry."
- **P6:** "The letters are cozy and creepy. The fights are just a menu."
- **P4:** "The fights are fine. I just want them faster."

## Limits
- **Greybox only.** Blocks with no art or music may make fights feel flatter than they would in the finished game.
- **One encounter design.** The session tested one turn-based system, not turn-based combat in general.
- **Self-selected players.** Newsletter readers chose the game for its letters, so they may dislike combat more than other players do.
