# Scope and Burn

The team has 14 months of runway at current spend, and five regions would take about 22 months to finish while three regions would take about 13. Only the three-region plan fits before the money runs out; the schedule rests on one slice's pace, so it is `[emerging]`.

## Method
- **Runway.** Ines divided the team's savings on 2026-09-05 by average monthly spend from June to August. "Burn" means monthly spend.
- **Pace.** The [[concepts/vertical-slice]] took about two months for most of Thimble Cross. Ines and Priya estimated each remaining region against that pace, adjusting for new art and the word counts in [[research/narrative/script-word-count]].
- **Shared work.** Systems, polish, porting checks, and launch tasks are counted once, outside any region.

## Findings
| Work | Five regions | Three regions |
|---|---|---|
| Shared systems, polish, and launch | 4 months | 4 months |
| Thimble Cross (finish) | 2 months | 2 months |
| Gloamwick | 3 months | 3 months |
| Sallow Ferry | 3 months | 3 months |
| The Drowned Bell | 5 months | Cut |
| The High Briar | 5 months | Cut |
| Moving the Drowned Bell's story and the High Briar's edges | None | 1 month |
| **Total** | **22 months** | **13 months** |

- **Runway is 14 months.** At current spend, savings last until about November 2027.
- **Five regions overrun by eight months.** The full plan needs about 22 months, eight more than the runway.
- **Three regions fit.** About 13 months of work lands in October 2027 with one month of runway to spare.
- **The cut regions cost the most.** The Drowned Bell needs flooded-chapel art and the High Briar a new thorn tileset, so each takes about five months.

## Limits
- **One data point for pace.** The schedule rests on one slice built by a team that was still learning the tools.
- **No slack.** The estimate has no buffer for illness, porting problems, or festival work.
- **Spend can change.** A grant, a publisher deal, or new costs would move the runway.
