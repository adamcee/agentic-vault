# Research

These docs hold Night Post's evidence: playtests, market data, accessibility and technical tests, and two summaries of outside standards. Each doc carries an `evidence-maturity` tag and ends with `## Reweave Triggers` ([[vault-guide/research-traceability]], [[vault-guide/research-paper-workflow]]).

Files starting with `_source-` are untouched AI originals kept beside the clean doc that passed the [[vault-guide/ingestion-gate]].

## Playtests

- [[research/playtests/pt-01-paper-prototype]]: 5 players; the letter-opening choice was the moment everyone remembered.
- [[research/playtests/pt-02-greybox-encounters]]: 6 players; turn-based encounters clashed with the tone.
- [[research/playtests/pt-03-parley-prototype]]: 8 players; 7 preferred parley. Tagged `supported`.
- [[research/playtests/pt-04-vertical-slice]]: 10 players; 6 got lost after the first moon-shift, and 4 squinted at letters.
- [[research/playtests/pt-05-demo-build]]: 12 players; landmarks cut getting lost to 2, and 5 used the font toggle.

## Market

- [[research/market/genre-comparables]]: eight comparable games at $15 to $25. Original: [[research/market/_source-genre-comparables]].
- [[research/market/community-survey-01]]: 214 responses on handhelds, price, and languages.
- [[research/market/publisher-pitch-feedback]]: three publishers; two said five regions was too much.
- [[research/market/demo-festival-results]]: 3,140 demo players, wishlists from 1,900 to 6,450, and 23 map requests.

## Accessibility

- [[research/accessibility/text-contrast-and-size-standards]]: WCAG 2.2 contrast targets and the Game Accessibility Guidelines. Tagged `established`.
- [[research/accessibility/night-palette-colorblind-test]]: red and green route lanterns looked the same under simulation.
- [[research/accessibility/letter-readability-audit]]: handwritten body text failed contrast and size at 800p.

## Technical

- [[research/technical/handheld-perf-benchmark]]: volumetric fog dropped Gloamwick to 38 fps; baked fog held 30.
- [[research/technical/save-corruption-test]]: direct writes corrupted 2 of 500 saves; temp-then-rename corrupted none.

## Narrative

- [[research/narrative/letter-length-test]]: letters under 120 words were read in full 88% of the time.
- [[research/narrative/script-word-count]]: 142,000 words for five regions, about 86,000 for three.

## Audio

- [[research/audio/field-recording-trip]]: two nights of wetland recording, 41 usable takes.
- [[research/audio/music-listening-test]]: 7 of 9 listeners tired of continuous music after about 20 minutes.

## Localization and Production

- [[research/localization/localization-cost-estimate]]: $0.11 to $0.16 per word from three vendors. Original: [[research/localization/_source-localization-cost-estimate]].
- [[research/production/scope-and-burn]]: 14 months of runway against 22 months for five regions or 13 for three.
