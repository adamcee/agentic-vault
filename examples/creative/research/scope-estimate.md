# Scope Estimate: The Branching Script Does Not Fit Two People; the Linear Script Does

The three-ending script needs about 95,000 words and 62 backgrounds, but the team can finish about 45,000 words and 600 art hours in the 30 weeks left. A linear script fits with room to spare.

*Admitted through the ingestion gate on 2026-09-21. Tightening removed an invented industry benchmark and corrected the background count from 58 to 62. Original: [[research/_source-scope-estimate]].*

## Rates Come from Three Weeks of Tracked Time
- **Backgrounds.** Teo painted 3 final backgrounds in 27 hours, so about 9 hours each.
- **Writing.** Mara finished about 1,500 words of dialogue per week alongside puzzle design.
- **Capacity.** Teo has 20 art hours a week, so 600 hours over 30 weeks. Mara's writing capacity is about 45,000 words.

Three weeks is a short baseline, so these rates are `[emerging]`.

## Branching Versus Linear

| Item | Branching (three endings) | Linear (one ending) |
|---|---|---|
| Scenes | 41 | 24 |
| Dialogue words | ~95,000 | ~40,000 |
| Backgrounds | 62 | 30 |
| Background hours | ~558 | ~270 |
| Background plus animation hours (animation ~150) | ~708 of 600 | ~420 of 600 |
| Writing weeks at 1,500 words a week | ~63 of 30 | ~27 of 30 |

## What the Numbers Mean
- **Branching overruns both people.** Art runs about 108 hours over, and writing needs twice the weeks available.
- **Linear leaves buffer.** About 180 art hours and 3 writing weeks remain for polish and playtest fixes.
