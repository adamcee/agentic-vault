# PDR-P002: Price Every Visit Separately

> **Superseded 2026-10-07** by [[decisions/product/PDR-P004-membership-plus-per-visit|PDR-P004]], which adds a monthly membership and keeps per-visit prices for non-members.

## Context
Riders need a simple price before the pilot starts on 2026-09-07. Local shops charge $55 to $95 for a basic tune-up, with a median of $70 ([[research/local-price-scan]] [emerging]).

## Decision
Every visit is priced on its own: a commuter service (check, clean, lube, and adjust) costs $55 at a workplace stop, and a full tune-up costs $85. Parts are extra at cost plus 30%.

## Alternatives Considered
- **Match shop prices at $70.** The van saves riders a trip, but a new business with no reviews needs a reason to try it.
- **Prepaid annual service plan.** Two local shops sell one ([[research/local-price-scan]] [emerging]), but nobody knows yet how often commuters will book.

## Consequences
- The founder estimates the $55 service at 40 minutes, which clears the $60-per-hour labor floor ([[architecture/CONSTITUTION]] P4).
- Revenue rises and falls with each week's bookings, with no baseline.

> **Reweave 2026-09-14 (strengthened):** 23 of 41 survey respondents preferred to pay per visit, against 12 who preferred a monthly plan ([[research/commuter-survey-2026-09]] [emerging]).
>
> **Reweave 2026-10-05 (weakened):** In the pilot, repeat customers rebooked every 20 to 27 days and compared each visit's price with shop prices, and three of four host workplaces asked for a monthly plan ([[research/pilot-month-results]] [emerging]). Flagged for the founder, with a superseding PDR proposed.
