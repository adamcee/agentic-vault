# PDR-P004: Monthly Membership Plus Per-Visit Pricing

## Context
The pilot month weakened per-visit pricing in [[decisions/product/PDR-P002-per-visit-pricing|PDR-P002]] on three points ([[research/pilot-month-results]] [emerging]):
- **Repeat riders already book monthly.** They rebooked every 20 to 27 days, and riders compared prices with shops on 11 of 52 visits.
- **Hosts want a plan they can pay for.** Three of four host workplaces asked for a monthly plan, and one asked about buying it for staff as a perk.
- **One-off riders still need a price.** 15 of 52 pilot visits were one-time jobs away from a workplace stop.

## Decision
Riders can join a $45 monthly membership that covers one commuter service a month at their workplace stop, free flat repairs, and 10% off parts. Non-members pay per visit: $60 for a commuter service (up from $55) and $85 for a full tune-up.

## Alternatives Considered
- **Keep per-visit pricing.** It leaves the price-shopping and the host requests unanswered.
- **Membership only.** It would drop the one-off and home-visit riders.
- **Four-visit punch card.** It is simpler to run, but it gives hosts no monthly bill to pay.

## Consequences
- Thirty members would bring a $1,350 monthly baseline toward the targets in [[strategy/business-goals]].
- A member who books every 24 days uses slightly more than one service a month, so members who book more often may need a second-visit price.
- The booking tool must support recurring monthly billing ([[decisions/technical/ADR-T001-online-booking-tool|ADR-T001]]).
