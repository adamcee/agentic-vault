# Van Day

A van day is one working day of the van, from loading in the morning to restocking at night. It is the unit the business uses to measure capacity and revenue.

- **Capacity.** One van day holds six commuter services or the equivalent mix of larger jobs, based on the founder's shop experience.
- **Revenue target.** [[strategy/business-goals]] sets $350 per van day as the full-time threshold.
- **Capacity test.** [[architecture/CONSTITUTION]] P1 allows premises or a second van only after eight weeks at 80% of capacity or more.
