# Weekend Stops at Apartment Buildings

Large apartment buildings with bike rooms could host weekend stops, the way workplaces host weekday ones. This would fill weekends without the driving cost of single home visits.

The idea needs a building manager willing to host and a test of whether residents book on weekends. It stays open until the membership launch in [[decisions/product/PDR-P004-membership-plus-per-visit|PDR-P004]] settles.
