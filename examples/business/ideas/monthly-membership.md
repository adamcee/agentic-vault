# Monthly Membership

Riders could pay a flat monthly fee that covers one service a month at their workplace. Twelve of 41 survey respondents preferred a monthly plan, mostly the five-day riders ([[research/commuter-survey-2026-09]] [emerging]).

Promoted on 2026-10-07 after the [[research/pilot-month-results|pilot month]] showed monthly rebooking and host requests for a plan.
