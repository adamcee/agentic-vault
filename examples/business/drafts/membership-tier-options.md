# Exploring Membership Tier Options for Curbside Cycles: A Deep Dive

## Overview

In this memo, we will delve into the various options that Curbside Cycles might consider as it moves forward with the exciting launch of its new monthly membership program. It is worth noting at the outset that membership design is a nuanced and multifaceted challenge, and there is no single right answer that will work for every business. That said, we believe the following analysis offers a robust framework for thinking through the key considerations.

## Why Tiers Matter

As previously discussed, the pilot month appears to suggest that repeat customers tend to rebook roughly every three to four weeks, which could potentially indicate a meaningful level of interest in a recurring offering. It is widely understood in the subscription economy that offering multiple tiers can help businesses capture a broader range of customers and maximize lifetime value, although of course results may vary depending on the specific context.

## Potential Tier Structures

The following table shows three possible tier structures that Curbside Cycles may wish to explore:

| Tier | Price | What's Included |
|---|---|---|
| Basic | $35/month | One safety check per month |
| Standard | $45/month | One commuter service per month, free flats, 10% off parts |
| Premium | $75/month | Two commuter services per month, free flats, 20% off parts, priority booking |

Each of these tiers offers a distinct value proposition, and together they could create a seamless upgrade path that encourages customers to move up over time. The Premium tier in particular could be a crucial driver of revenue, as it would allow Curbside Cycles to leverage its most loyal customers.
