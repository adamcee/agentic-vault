# Commuter Survey, September 2026

Most bike commuters surveyed would use repair done at their workplace, and most preferred to pay per visit. The founder collected 41 responses from 2026-09-08 to 2026-09-12, one round with a self-selected sample, so every finding here is `[emerging]`.

## Method
- **Where.** A QR-code form posted in the bike rooms of two office parks, plus one post in an online group for local bike commuters.
- **Who.** 41 adults who ride a bike at least once a week. Respondents chose to answer, so keen riders are likely over-represented.
- **What.** Ten questions on riding habits, repair delays, workplace service, fair price, and how they would prefer to pay.

## Findings
| Question | Result (n = 41) |
|---|---|
| Ride to work four or more days a week | 29 |
| Put off a repair because a shop visit means days without the bike | 26 |
| Would use repair done at work during work hours | 31 |
| Fair price for a check, clean, lube, and adjust | Median $50, range $30–$80 |
| Prefer to pay per visit | 23 |
| Prefer a monthly plan | 12 |
| No preference | 6 |

- **Workplace service is the draw.** Three in four respondents would use it, and the delay of a shop visit was the most common reason given for putting off repairs.
- **Per-visit payment led, but not by much.** Twelve respondents preferred a monthly plan, and nine of those twelve rode five days a week.

## Limits
The office-park bike rooms favor riders whose employers already support cycling. The fair-price question named no brand or quality level.
