# Local Price Scan: Six Nearby Bike Shops

Six bike shops within 5 km of downtown charge $55 to $95 for a basic tune-up, with a median of $70, and none of them repairs bikes on site. The founder phoned each shop and checked its website on 2026-08-31, one round only, so the findings are `[emerging]`.

## Prices and Turnaround

| Shop | Basic tune-up | Flat repair (with tube) | Turnaround quoted | Service plan |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| A | $55 | $15 | 5–7 days | None |
| B | $60 | $14 | 3–4 days | Annual, $120 for two tune-ups |
| C | $70 | $18 | 2–3 days | None |
| D | $70 | $16 | 4–5 days | None |
| E | $85 | $20 | 2–3 days | Annual, $199 for unlimited adjustments |
| F | $95 | $20 | 3–5 days | None |

## Findings
- **Price range.** A basic tune-up costs $55 to $95, median $70.
- **Turnaround.** Every shop quoted two days or more, and the busiest quoted a week.
- **No on-site service.** No shop repairs bikes at the rider's home or workplace, and one offers pickup for $25.
- **Plans exist but are rare.** Two of six shops sell a prepaid annual plan.

## Limits
The scan covers one city area on one day in late summer. Prices quoted by phone may differ from final bills once parts are added.
