# Send Staff SMS Links Instead of Asking Them to Install an App

Staff could get each shift change as an SMS with a link to a phone web page, with no app and no password. Round 1 of the manager interviews suggested staff will not install another work app ([[research/manager-interview-synthesis]]).

Promoted on 2026-09-10 into [[decisions/technical/ADR-T002-responsive-web-pwa|ADR-T002]] after round 2 confirmed the finding.
