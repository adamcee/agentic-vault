# Tip-Pooling Calculator

Brigade already knows who worked which hours, so it could split a night's pooled tips by hours worked. Three of five generic apps bundle pay or tip features ([[research/competitor-feature-scan]]).

Rejected on 2026-09-23. Tip rules vary by state and by restaurant, a wrong split creates a wage dispute, and clause P3 of [[architecture/CONSTITUTION]] keeps Brigade out of pay.
