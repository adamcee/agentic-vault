# Shift Swap

A shift swap is a request by one staff member to hand a scheduled shift to a coworker, who must accept it before it goes further. Brigade treats a one-way handoff and a two-way trade of shifts as the same kind of swap.

- **Same-role swap:** both people hold the shift's role, such as two servers or two line cooks.
- **Cross-role swap:** the coworker does not hold the shift's role, such as a server taking a bartender shift.

Under [[decisions/product/PDR-P002-manager-approval-for-swaps|PDR-P002]], every swap needs manager approval. [[ideas/auto-approve-same-role-swaps]] proposes skipping approval for same-role swaps that add no overtime.
