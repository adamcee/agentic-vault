# Location

A location is one physical restaurant with its own staff list and its own weekly schedule. It is Brigade's billing unit under [[decisions/product/PDR-P001-flat-fee-per-location|PDR-P001]].

- Two restaurants under one owner are two locations, even when some staff work at both.
- A restaurant with a separate bar or patio schedule at the same address is still one location.
